The Norix Attenda Wall-Mounted Desk is designed for rooms where floor space is limited and is well suited to adult and juvenile detention settings. It mounts to a solid wall, is ADA compliant, and is available with or without a decorative high-pressure laminate top.

The base is rotationally molded from high-impact polyethylene and can be filled with rigid polyurethane foam for sound absorption. The ATN625 adds a laminate top fastened with a steel-to-steel connection and finished with a rounded molded edge. The desk is tested to a 1,000 lb static load.

  • Construction: Rotationally molded high-impact polyethylene base with UV stabilizers
  • Overall Dimensions: 35.5” W × 23.7” D × 23.8” H (no laminate) or 24.8” H (laminate)
  • Accessibility: ADA compliant
  • Base Colors: Twilight, Cove, Sand, Sienna, Dove
  • Top Laminates (ATN625): Kensington Maple, Wild Cherry, Asian Sun, Montana Walnut, Palisades Oak
  • Static Load Testing: Tested to 1,000 lbs
  • Flammability Standards: California Technical Bulletin 133 and 117, UL 1056, ASTM E1537, and NFPA 261
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD and GREENGUARD Gold
  • Warranty: 10-year limited replacement warranty
  • Intended Use: Indoor use only
  • Country of Manufacture: USA

Available Models and Options

  • ATN625 – With Laminate Top: 24.8” H
  • ATN620 – Without Laminate Top: 23.8” H
  • ATN621 – Without Laminate Top and Without Bottom Cores: 23.8” H

Installation and Options

  • Wall-Mounted Installation: Designed to be mounted to a solid wall. Anchors are required and are not supplied by Norix. A groove along the mounting surface accepts security caulk (caulk not supplied).
  • Assembly: None required

Applications

The Norix Attenda Wall-Mounted Desk gives residents a work surface while keeping floor space open, which is especially useful in smaller detention and behavioral health rooms.

Typical applications include:

  • Behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Correctional and detention facilities
  • Other supervised residential and institutional settings

About Norix Attenda Molded Bedroom Series

The Norix Attenda Series is a line of molded bedroom furniture for behavioral healthcare and correctional facilities. It includes beds, nightstands, open chests, desks, a stool, wall-mounted storage units, and a wardrobe, all molded from high-impact polyethylene in a clean, contemporary style. Each piece is designed to attach flush to the wall or floor.

Common characteristics of the Attenda Series include:

  • Molded Construction: Rotationally molded high-impact polyethylene with smooth, rounded edges
  • Ligature-Resistant Design: Molded shapes without pass-throughs or tie-off points to help reduce ligature risk
  • Contraband-Resistant Design: No creases or nooks, which helps reduce places to hide contraband
  • Easy Cleaning: Chemically resistant surfaces that can be cleaned with bleach solutions and hospital-grade disinfectants
  • TruColor™ Technology: Fully compounded color for a consistent finish without streaks or speckles
