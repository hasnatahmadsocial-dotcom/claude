The Norix Max-Master 6 Seat Rectangle Table is a 30”×72” table with 6 attached seats, built for the daily demands of dining halls and dayrooms in correctional and behavioral healthcare facilities.

Each seat has its own leg, which gives the table more stability than a single pedestal base. The legs are 3” diameter, 14-gauge steel tubing with a 10” radius bend that makes it easy to clean under the table. The seats are high-density, color-impregnated composite that resists scratches and stains, and Torx® tamper-resistant hardware holds everything together. Tables can be bolted to the floor or set on tamper-resistant glides.

  • Legs: 3” diameter, 14-gauge curved steel tubing with fully welded 6”×6”×¼” steel mounting plates
  • Seats: 13” diameter Super Slammer Stone compression-molded composite, scratch and stain resistant
  • Top: High-pressure laminate over a 45 lb density M3 particle board core, with a 1½” thick radiused molded edge that is pressure fused to the top
  • Top Attachment: Embedded T-nuts give a steel-to-steel connection between the top and legs
  • Top Size: 30”×72”
  • Overall Footprint: 63”×72”
  • Heights: 31.8” table top, 18.9” seat
  • Weight: 280 lbs
  • Plain Top Finishes: Titanium Evolve, Wild Cherry, Kensington Maple, Asian Sun, Montana Walnut, Palisades Oak, Calcutta Marble, or Black Alicante laminate with a matching or black molded edge
  • Stainless Steel Top: Optional 10-gauge 304 stainless steel with a 1½” drop edge, which changes the height to 30.5”
  • Seat Colors: Twilight, Cove, Sand, Raven, or stainless steel
  • Leg Colors: Black or metallic silver
  • Static Load Testing: Tested to 1,000 lbs
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD and GREENGUARD Gold
  • Warranty: 10-year limited replacement warranty
  • Intended Use: Indoor use only
  • Country of Manufacture: USA

Available Models and Options

  • M63072 – Laminate Plain Top: 280 lbs

Installation and Options

  • Bolt-Down or Glides: Bolt to the floor with the concealed concrete expansion anchors included, or use the optional tamper-resistant leg glides. Glides cannot be added in the field to a table ordered for bolt-down.
  • Clear Leg Protectors: Optional clear PVC protectors, 3” diameter, 6” high, specified when ordering
  • Stainless Steel Seats: Optional 12” stainless steel seats, which lower the seat height to 18.25”
  • ADA Version: Available for bolt-down tables, with the seating arranged for wheelchair access
  • Assembly: Secure the base to the top and attach the seats; see Norix instructions

Applications

The Norix Max-Master 6 Seat Rectangle Table suits dining halls and dayrooms where fixed seating and a sturdy table need to hold up to constant use.

Typical applications include:

  • Dayrooms and dining halls in correctional and detention facilities
  • Behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Visitation areas, classrooms, and other common areas

About Norix Dayroom and Dining Tables

Norix tables are made for dayrooms, dining halls, classrooms, and visitation areas in correctional and behavioral healthcare facilities, where furniture is used hard every day. The range covers molded-base tables such as the Jupiter and Molded X-Base, laminate X-Base tables, and steel-base tables such as the Leg Style, Multi-Purpose, Max-Master, and Oasis.

Common characteristics of Norix tables include:

  • Durable Tops: High-pressure laminate over a dense particle board core, with molded or T-mold edges depending on the model
  • Secure Connections: Tops attach to the base with tamper-resistant hardware for a solid metal-to-metal connection
  • Placement Options: Depending on the model, tables can be ballasted, bolted to the floor, or set on tamper-resistant glides
