The key to Attenda’s strength and durability is its advanced construction. Molded
in specially formulated, high-impact polyethylene, each Attenda® product is
structurally enhanced for increased strength and durability. They are solid as a
rock, yet feature seamless construction with rounded corners for added safety and
security. Each piece is designed to be attached flush to the wall or floor.
