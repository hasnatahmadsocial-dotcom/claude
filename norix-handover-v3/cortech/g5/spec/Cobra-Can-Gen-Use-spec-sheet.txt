 OVERVIEW
 The Cobra Can Food Grade/General Use Waste Container is virtually UNBREAKABLE and is the toughest, and most innovative
 trash receptacle. Ideal for continuous use in extreme environments! Manufactured to ISO Quality Standards in the USA.

 FABRICATION/MATERIAL
 Rotationally molded structural polyethylene for maximum strength while maintaining flexibility. 360° molded in continuous
 handles surround the entire can perimeter and underside for easier lifting. Molded in floor glides reduce floor scratching
 when dragged across the floor.
