 OVERVIEW
 The Kangaroo Cart is an innovative cart that delivers unmatched durability, versatility and maneuverability. Ideal for continuous
 use in extreme environments. Uses include: Laundry Collection & Delivery, Garbage Collection, Recycling Bin, General Delivery
 Cart & Material Handling, Food Processing, Janitor Cart and can be used Indoors or Outdoors. Manufactured to ISO Quality
 Standards in the USA.

 FABRICATION/MATERIAL
 Durable, one piece, rotationally molded design for increased strength. Constructed of high impact, no break, polyethylene. Load
 Rating/15 Bushel Capacity, Heavy Duty Casters Secured with Stainless Steel, Tamper-Proof Hardware, Push – Pull Handles On
 Both Sides that Accommodate Utility Use, Drain Plug for Easy Cleaning, Manufactured Three Ways: Flame Retardant (Passes
 Cal 133), General Use and Recycled Material. Call for FR test results,
