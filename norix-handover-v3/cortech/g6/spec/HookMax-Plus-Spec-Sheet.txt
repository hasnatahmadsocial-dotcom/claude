 Ligature Resistant Hook - Releases at Maximum 20 lb Load




• Releases at maximum 20 lb load
• 360° release

Despite the hook’s “normalized” design, the unique choice of materials combined with
specific design details make it both safe and functional. The variety of colors offer the
opportunity for a tailored interior design.

The hook holds the weight of an item of clothing or a towel, but releases when an extra
force is applied. The hook does not break, it bends and forces the ligature to slide off. It
releases in all directions and can be placed close to furniture, doors, etc.
