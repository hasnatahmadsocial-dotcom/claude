                                                                                              SOLSTICE®
                                                                                             Safeguard Bed
The Solstice® Safeguard Bed emulates the therapeutic appearance of our Solstice® Bed with added features to
support the safety of Fall Risk Patients. Raised, molded-in wall panels run laterally along each side of the bed to
help prevent injury by falling. The partial paneling contributes to patient comfort and security without restricting
movement entirely.
