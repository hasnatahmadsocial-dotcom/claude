             Steel Leg Tables - Bolt Down Instructions
• Lay the table upside down, so the bolt locations are exposed.
• Attach all legs.
• Each leg will come with a single bracket (2 pictures below), and bolt.




• Each table leg will have a threaded hole in the center, inset the bracket as shown below.




• Position the outside tab in the customers preferred orientation. Eg. Pointing away from the table,
  or pointing towards the center of the table. Then tighten down the bolt.
• Bolt down hole shown as pointed out below.




• After installing all bolt down brackets, ﬂip the table over to its normal standing position.
  Proceed to mark the ﬂoor in the locations of the bolt down holes.




• Drill out the 4 holes in the ﬂoor, reposition the table and use customer preferred
  expansion anchors to attach to the ﬂoor.
