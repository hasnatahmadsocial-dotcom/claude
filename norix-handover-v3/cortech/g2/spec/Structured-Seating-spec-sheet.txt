OVERVIEW
Structured Seating is designed specifically for waiting rooms, lobbies, and common areas. The ergonomic design provides
maximum comfort using rotational molding technology and is virtually indestructible. Manufactured to ISO Quality Standards
in the USA.

FABRICATION/MATERIAL
Seat: Durable, one piece, rotationally molded design for increased strength. Constructed of high impact “no break” polyethylene.
Molded-in threaded steel inserts provide for tamper-resistant, “steel-to-steel” attachment to base frame with tamper proof hardware.
Hardware bit size: T40.
Frame: Continuous seam welded frame structure, 2” diameter, 14 gauge powder coated steel with 11 gauge steel seat supports.
COLOR: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast,
Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray and Tide. Custom colors available upon request.
WEIGHT(s): Contact Cortech for Details
OPTIONS:
   Multiple Configurations from 1 to 4 seats, with and without arms, variable table placement.
   Frames are available with tamper resistant, nylon-bottom glides or bolt to floor options.
WARRANTY: 3 Year Factory Warranty.
MAINTENANCE: Easy to clean, sanitize & maintain.
