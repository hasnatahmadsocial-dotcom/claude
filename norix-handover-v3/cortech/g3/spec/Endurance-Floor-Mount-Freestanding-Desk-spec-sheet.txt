OVERVIEW
The Endurance® Freestanding Desk is a versatile, heavy duty, desk that is ideal for continuous, intensive use
environments. Manufactured to ISO Quality Standards in the USA.

FABRICATION/MATERIAL
Durable, one piece, rotationally molded design for increased strength. Constructed of high impact polyethylene. No steel or
wood components. Flame Retardant (as shown passes Cal 133). Test reports available upon request.
COLOR Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast,
Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray and Tide. Custom colors available upon request. See Color Guide for laminate
options.

WEIGHT 47 lbs.
ASSEMBLY Bolt to the floor via concealed pilot holes with hardware of your choice.
WARRANTY All Plastic: Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects. Laminate Top: 5 year warranty against
manufacturing defects.
MAINTENANCE Easy to clean, sanitize & maintain.
