Endurance® Nightstand

                         You will need:




                           1x                         1x                  8x                       8x
                           Pencil For Marking Floor   Drill             3/8-16x3.75" Security    Flat Washer
                           And Wall                   With 3/8 Bit      Torx Anchor Bolts




                        1.    Position nightstand on its side as to access the bottom of the nightstand.




                        2. Select and drill all eight mounting locations on the bottom and
                           back the nightstand.


                        3. Place stand right side up and locate where it is to be secured to the ﬂoor.
                        4. Using a pen or pencil, mark mounting hole locations on the ﬂoor
                           and wall using the holes drilled in step 2.



                        5. Move nightstand and drill holes in the eight locations where you
                           marked the ﬂoor and wall. Hole depth should be 2.0" deep MINIMUM.



                         6. Replace the nightstand lining up the drilled holes with the holes in
                            the ﬂoor and wall.



                        7. guide
                               Place one ﬂat washer over each of the eight anchor bolt and
                                 the bolts through the nightstand and into the holes into
                           the ﬂoor.


                        8. Tighten each anchor bolt 3-5 turns past ﬁnger tight.
