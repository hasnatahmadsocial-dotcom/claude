OVERVIEW
The toughest locking property storage locker, is specifically designed to exceed all requirements of a property storage
locker system. Built with a flush mounted Masterlock combination lock with center key override. Manufactured to ISO
Quality Standards in the USA.
FABRICATION/MATERIAL
Durable, rotationally molded design for increased strength. Constructed with high impact “no break” polyethylene.
No wood components. Flame Retardant (as shown passes Cal 133). Test reports available upon request.

Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray and Tide. Custom colors available upon request.
