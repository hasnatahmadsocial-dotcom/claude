  OVERVIEW
  The EZ Bunk® is designed and manufactured for frequent use as a temporary sleep surface. Durable, stackable/ space saving,
  clean, humane, ergonomic, cost effective, flame retardant, functional and aesthetically pleasing. The EZ Bunk® has no seams or
  holes, designed with carrying handles on sides and ends and includes internal molded structural cones for comfort and support.
  The EZ Bunk® also fits easily under most wall mounted bunks. Manufactured to ISO Quality Standards in the USA.

  FABRICATION/MATERIAL
  Durable, one piece, rotationally molded design for increased strength. Constructed with high density polyethylene with
  structural and flame retardant additives. Flame retardant (passes CAL TB133). Call Cortech for test results.
