 OVERVIEW
 The Barracuda Box is stronger, lighter, and more functional than other property storage boxes. Manufactured to ISO Quality
 Standards in the USA.

 FABRICATION/MATERIAL
 Constructed of high impact, no break, polypropylene. Flame Retardant (Passes CAL133).Test results available upon request.

 “Stack and Nest” Design - Stack property boxes with or without a lid without crushing the contents of the Barracuda Box. “Nest”
 property boxes inside one another by simply turning the empty box 180 degrees. Save on freight and save storage space.
 Radius design (rounded edges and corners) for maximum impact dispersion and resistance. Vertical radiused structural columns
 for maximum strength and impact resistance. Locking hasp on both sides of the box.

 COLOR Gray. Custom colors available upon request
 WARRANTY 10 Year Factory Warranty.
 MAINTENANCE Easy to clean, sanitize & maintain.
