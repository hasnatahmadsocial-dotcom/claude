OVERVIEW
Tiger plates are injection molded with a heavy-duty polycarbonate blend and designed for continuous use in high-traffic areas. The molding
system eliminates the need for painting and the oversized plates allow for mounting directly into any wall type. Manufactured to ISO Quality
Standards in the USA.

FABRICATION/MATERIAL
Injection molded in high strength Polycarbonate, the TP series will not rust or break. Our molding system eliminates the need for painting. All
plates are oversized to accommodate mounting directly into any wall type. The oversized design allows for j-box opening and mounting surface
imperfections.
