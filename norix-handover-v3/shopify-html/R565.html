The Norix Polycarbonate Wall Mirror is a lightweight, impact-resistant mirror for behavioral health and correctional facilities, where glass is not safe. It mounts flush to the wall, which eliminates pass-throughs and tie-off points to reduce ligature risk, and it comes in small (R565-416) and large (R565-417) sizes.

Each mirror is extruded from optical quality polycarbonate made from 100% virgin Lexan resin, for a clear image with outstanding strength. A durable coating protects the mirrored second surface, so the mirror can be handled and installed without scratching.

  • Material: Polycarbonate extruded from 100% virgin Lexan resin, in an ISO 8 clean room with inline optical scanning
  • Mirrored Surface: Tamper-resistant second-surface mirror, protected by a durable coating against scratches
  • Ligature Resistant: The flush-to-wall design eliminates pass-throughs and tie-offs
  • Impact Resistant: Engineered for outstanding strength in high-traffic and corrections environments
  • Sizes: 9.5”W×11.3”H×¼”D (small) or 11.3”W×17.3”H×¼”D (large)
  • Weight: 1 lb (small) or 2 lbs (large)
  • Cleaning: Microfiber cloth with non-abrasive soap and water; do not use glass cleaner
  • Warranty: 1-year limited replacement warranty; does not cover damage from improper installation, maintenance, accident, or misuse
  • Intended Use: Indoor use only

Available Models and Options

  • R565-416 – Small: 9.5”W×11.3”H×¼”D, 1 lb
  • R565-417 – Large: 11.3”W×17.3”H×¼”D, 2 lbs; compatible with the Norix Arcadian Wardrobe

Installation and Options

  • Mounting Surface: Norix recommends mounting to solid concrete surfaces; for other surfaces, consult a professional installer
  • Screws: Do not overtighten the screws, and avoid bevel and flat head screws, which can damage the polycarbonate. Installation hardware not included

Applications

The Norix Polycarbonate Wall Mirror gives facilities a safe alternative to glass in rooms where residents are not always supervised.

Typical applications include:

  • Patient rooms and bathrooms in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Inmate cells and washrooms in correctional and detention facilities
  • Rooms furnished with the Norix Arcadian Wardrobe, which the large mirror fits
