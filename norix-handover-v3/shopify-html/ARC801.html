The Norix Arcadian Wardrobe With Door is a one-piece, rotationally molded wardrobe with a molded door for behavioral health patient rooms. It gives residents a homelike, private place to store clothing and belongings, with the durability of high-impact polyethylene. The seamless design has no internal openings, pass-throughs, or tie-off points, which reduces ligature risk.

Inverted internal support ribs give the wardrobe its strength without interior foam, and concealed, tamper-resistant hardware anchors it to the floor. The door comes with a lock as standard, hinges on the left or right, and can be fitted with a Norix steel or polycarbonate safety mirror. The slightly textured surface can be cleaned with bleach solutions and hospital-grade disinfectants.

  • Construction: One-piece, rotationally molded high-impact polyethylene with no internal openings
  • Support: Inverted internal support ribs for strength, with no interior foam
  • Surface: Slightly textured surface for long-term, maintenance-free performance
  • Chemical Resistance: Resists blood, vinegar, urine, feces, salt solution, and chlorine solution
  • Door: Molded door with a standard lock, hinged on the left or right
  • Overall Size: 24”W×24.3”D×78”H
  • Weight: 182 lbs
  • Colors: Twilight, Cove, Sage, Sand, Sienna, or Dove
  • Flammability Standards: California Technical Bulletins 133 and 117; ASTM E1537; NFPA 261
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD and GREENGUARD Gold
  • Cleaning: Simple Green or a similar cleaner for routine cleaning; bleach and water solutions and hospital-grade disinfectants such as Virex, Wexcide, or Oxivir Five 16 can be used per the manufacturer’s instructions
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty
  • Recyclable: 100% recyclable
  • Intended Use: Indoor use only
  • Country of Manufacture: USA

Installation and Options

  • Bolt-Down: Bolt-down kit required for floor anchoring. The Norix anchoring system uses concealed hardware and is intended for concrete floors and/or walls
  • Security Caulk: May be applied around the perimeter after anchoring
  • Assembly: None required

Applications

The Norix Arcadian Wardrobe With Door suits patient rooms where residents need private, lockable storage that is safe and still feels like home.

Typical applications include:

  • Patient rooms in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Residential treatment centers
  • Rooms in correctional and detention facilities

About Norix Arcadian Bedroom Furniture

The Norix Arcadian Series brings a residential look to behavioral health patient rooms, with furniture rotationally molded from a specially formulated, high-impact polyethylene that stands up to intensive use.

Common characteristics of Arcadian furniture include:

  • Ligature Resistant Design: One-piece, seamless construction eliminates pass-throughs and tie-offs
  • Concealed Hardware: Floor anchoring with concealed, tamper-resistant hardware
  • Contraband Resistant: No creases or nooks to hide contraband when bolted down
  • Infection Control: Seamless surfaces are impervious to fluids and can be cleaned with bleach solutions and hospital-grade disinfectants
  • Inverted Support Ribs: Internal ribs add strength without interior foam
  • Environmentally Responsible: Every piece is 100% recyclable

The Arcadian Series includes a platform bed, nightstand, floor-mount desk, open chest, two shelf units, and wardrobes with or without a molded door.
