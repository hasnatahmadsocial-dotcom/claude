The Cortech Master Box Inmate Property Storage Box Lid closes off a Cortech Master Box. The placement of the locking hasp hole helps prevent tampering and keeps liquids from being poured into the box, and the lid slides open from the short side so staff can reach more of the contents without taking it off.

The lids are molded from high impact “no break” polypropylene, with a full perimeter stacking groove for stacking boxes. There are two sizes: one for the 1.5 and 2.0 cu ft boxes, and one for the 3.0 cu ft box. The box is sold separately.

  • Material: High impact “no break” polypropylene
  • Handles: Molded-in handles for opening and closing
  • Access: Slides open on the short side of the box
  • Security: Hasp hole placement helps prevent tampering and liquids being poured in; full engagement edges on three sides help prevent prying when the box is locked
  • Stacking: Full perimeter stacking groove for stacking boxes
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133
  • Color: Gray (custom colors available on request)
  • Box: Sold separately as the Cortech Master Box
  • Warranty: 10-year warranty
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 9689M – Lid for 1.5 and 2.0 cu ft Boxes: Fits the 3825M and 7268M boxes, 23½”W×17½”D×1½”H
  • 8289M – Lid for 3.0 cu ft Box: Fits the 8568M box, 29”W×18½”D×1½”H

No assembly is required.

Applications

Adding a lid lets facilities close off Master Boxes and stack them neatly, whether the boxes are kept in cells or in a property room.

Typical applications include:

  • Prisons, jails, and detention centers
  • Allowable in-cell property storage
  • Inmate records and personal property storage

About Cortech Property Storage Boxes

Cortech property storage boxes give jails, prisons, and other secure facilities a durable way to store inmate property and records. Both box lines are molded from high impact “no break” polypropylene in the same three sizes.

Cortech property storage includes:

  • Barracuda Box: Stack-and-nest property boxes in 1.5, 2.0, and 3.0 cu ft sizes, with a locking hasp on both sides
  • Master Box: Lockable property boxes in the same three sizes, with a built-in combination dial lock and master override key
  • Lids: Matching Barracuda Box and Master Box lids in two sizes, sold separately
