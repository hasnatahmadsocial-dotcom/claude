The Cortech Endurance Floor Mounted Stool gives correctional and other high-security facilities fixed, tamper-resistant seating. It is most often installed in cells with a desk, and it can also be used in dayrooms, intake areas, and other spaces where seating has to stay in place.

The stool is rotationally molded in one piece from high impact “no break” polyethylene, with no steel or wood components. It bolts to the floor through concealed pilot holes with hardware of your choice, and its rounded edges and smooth surfaces are easy to clean.

  • Material: One-piece rotationally molded high impact “no break” polyethylene; no steel or wood components
  • Dimensions: 16½”W×16½”D×15½”H
  • Weight: 8 lbs
  • Mounting: Bolts to the floor through concealed pilot holes (hardware not included)
  • Antimicrobial Testing: Tested to ISO 22196, showing a greater than 99% reduction in bacteria
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133 and UL 94 HB
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD Gold
  • Easy Cleaning: Sound dampening, and easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 7901 – Floor Mounted Stool: 8 lbs; desk sold separately

Installation and Options

  • Floor Mounting: Drill four mounting holes through the bottom of the stool, then fasten it to the floor with four anchor bolts and flat washers in holes at least 2” deep; see Cortech instructions
  • Hardware: Not included; use hardware of your choice

Applications

The Floor Mounted Stool suits cells and other secure spaces where seating must stay in place and resist tampering.

Typical applications include:

  • Cells and housing units in correctional facilities and jails
  • Intake and holding areas
  • Dayrooms and other high-security spaces
