The Cortech Endurance Crew Armless Chair is a compact molded chair for dining halls, dayrooms, and group spaces in behavioral healthcare and correctional facilities. The armless profile saves space and makes flexible layouts easy.

The chair is rotationally molded in one piece from high impact “no break” polyethylene, with no metal or wood components and no gaps or fasteners to tamper with. It can stand alone, be weighted at the factory or filled on site, or be bolted to the floor. The Crew Armless Chair is rated for outdoor use.

  • Material: One-piece rotationally molded high impact “no break” polyethylene; no steel or wood components
  • Dimensions: 22”W×26”D×32”H
  • Weight: 30 lbs
  • Weighting: Can be factory weighted or filled on site with approved material; do not use loose sand
  • Floor Mounting: Optional bolt-down kit for bolting to the floor
  • Outdoor Use: Rated for outdoor use and UV stabilized
  • Antimicrobial Testing: Tested to ISO 22196, showing a greater than 99% reduction in bacteria
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133 and UL 94 HB
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD Gold
  • Easy Cleaning: Sound dampening, and easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 6484 – Standard: 22”W×26”D×32”H, 30 lbs
  • 6484S – With Access Door: Armless chair with an access door
  • 6484KT – With Bolt-Down Kit: Armless chair with the floor bracket for bolting to the floor

Installation and Options

  • Floor Mounting: The optional floor bracket attaches to the chair with tamper-resistant Torx screws, then bolts to the floor; see Cortech instructions. Hardware not included
  • Weighting: Factory weighted or filled on site with approved material; do not use loose sand

Applications

The Crew Armless Chair suits dining and group areas that need safe, durable seating that is easy to rearrange.

Typical applications include:

  • Dining halls and dayrooms in correctional facilities and jails
  • Group rooms and dayrooms in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Residential treatment centers and other group spaces
