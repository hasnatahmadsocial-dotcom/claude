The Cortech Endurance Wedge Table is a compact, angled end and corner table for dayrooms, lounges, and shared spaces in behavioral healthcare, correctional, and other secure facilities. Its wedge shape fits beside chairs or between seating groups, and it can be combined with any seating style.

The table is rotationally molded in one piece from high impact “no break” polyethylene, with no steel or wood components and no seams to tamper with. It can be bolted to the floor with the optional bolt-down kit, and the 484S has underside ballast doors for adding weight with sandbags. The Wedge Table is rated for outdoor use.

  • Material: One-piece rotationally molded high impact “no break” polyethylene; no steel or wood components
  • Dimensions: 27”W×19”D×16½”H
  • Weight: 13 lbs
  • Ballast: The 484S has underside ballast doors for adding weight with sandbags, secured with tamper-resistant screws
  • Outdoor Use: Rated for outdoor use and UV stabilized
  • Antimicrobial Testing: Tested to ISO 22196, showing a greater than 99% reduction in bacteria
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133 and UL 94 HB
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD Gold
  • Easy Cleaning: Sound dampening, and easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 484 – Standard: Without access door, 13 lbs
  • 484S – With Access Door: Underside ballast doors for adding sandbags
  • 484KIT – Bolt-Down Kit: Floor attachment for bolting the Wedge Table to the floor with hardware of your choice, sold separately

Installation and Options

  • Floor Mounting: Bolt to the floor through concealed pilot holes with the optional bolt-down kit and hardware of your choice
  • Ballast: Add sandbags through the 484S ballast doors; Cortech says not to use loose sand

Applications

The Wedge Table suits dayrooms and lounges where a small table has to fit beside chairs or into corners and stand up to hard daily use.

Typical applications include:

  • Dayrooms and lounges in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Dayrooms and common areas in correctional facilities and jails
  • Waiting rooms and residential treatment centers
