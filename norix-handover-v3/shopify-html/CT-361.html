The Cortech Summit All Purpose Table is a dining height table with a molded base and a laminate top, for dining areas, dayrooms, and activity rooms in behavioral healthcare and other secure settings. It comes with a 42” round top or a medium or large delta top.

The one-piece molded base has no exposed seams. The base can be filled with sandbags, and the table can also be bolted to the floor.

  • Base: One-piece rotationally molded high impact polyethylene; no steel or wood components
  • Top: High-pressure laminate top with a Dura-Edge, in Gray Nebula with a black edge; it resists moisture and impact and cleans easily
  • Sizes: 42” round top (361 / 361S), medium delta top (363 / 363S), or large delta top (364 / 364S)
  • Dimensions: 42”W×42”D×29”H (42” round top) or 44”W×43”D×29”H (delta top)
  • Weight: Base 16 lbs; delta top 45 lbs; 42” round top 56 lbs
  • Game Top: Optional game top for the 42” round table (361GT)
  • Weighting: The base can be filled with sandbags; do not use loose sand
  • Floor Mounting: Optional concealed mounting
  • Flammability Standards: Laminate surfaces pass CAL 117; the base is flame retardant and passes California Technical Bulletin 133
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD Gold (laminate tops not included)
  • Easy Cleaning: Sound dampening, and easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Base Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty on the base; 5-year warranty on the laminate top, against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 361 – 42” Round Plain Top: Gray Nebula laminate, black edge
  • 361S – 42” Round Plain Top with Ballast Door: Gray Nebula laminate, black edge
  • 361GT – 42” Round Game Top: Game top
  • 363 – Medium Delta Top: Gray Nebula laminate, black edge
  • 363S – Medium Delta Top with Ballast Door: Gray Nebula laminate, black edge
  • 364 – Large Delta Top: Gray Nebula laminate, black edge
  • 364S – Large Delta Top with Ballast Door: Gray Nebula laminate, black edge

Installation and Options

  • Assembly: The base attaches to the top with tamper-resistant screws; hardware bit size is T40
  • Floor Mounting: Optional concealed mounting with stud plates; concrete anchors and security Torx bolts are included; see Cortech instructions
  • Weighting: Add sandbags through the ballast door in the base on the 361S, 363S, and 364S; do not use loose sand. Ballast door bit size is T27

Applications

The Summit All Purpose Table suits dining and activity areas that want a durable table with a calm, residential look.

Typical applications include:

  • Dining rooms and dayrooms in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Activity and group rooms in residential treatment centers
  • Other secure settings that serve meals or hold group activities
