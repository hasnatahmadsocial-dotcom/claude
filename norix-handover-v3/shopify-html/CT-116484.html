The Cortech Sentinel Armless Chair is a compact molded chair for dayrooms, patient rooms, and group spaces in behavioral healthcare and correctional facilities. The armless profile saves space, and the chair can stand alone or be bolted to other Sentinel chairs for group seating.

The chair is rotationally molded in one piece from high impact “no break” polyethylene, with no steel or wood components, and can be ballasted with sand for safety (model 116484S).

  • Material: One-piece rotationally molded high impact “no break” polyethylene; no steel or wood components
  • Dimensions: 24”W×24”D×33”H
  • Weight: 30 lbs
  • Ganging: Can be bolted together with other Sentinel chairs for group seating
  • Floor Mounting: Can be bolted to the floor through concealed pilot holes
  • Ballast Door: The 116484S has a ballast door for adding sandbags on site; it closes with tamper-resistant screws. Do not use loose sand
  • Outdoor Use: Rated for outdoor use and UV stabilized
  • Antimicrobial Testing: Tested to ISO 22196, showing a greater than 99% reduction in bacteria
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133 and UL 94 HB
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD Gold
  • Easy Cleaning: Sound dampening, and easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 116484 – Standard: Without access door
  • 116484S – With Access Door: Ballast door for adding sandbags on site

Installation and Options

  • Floor Mounting: Bolt to the floor through concealed pilot holes with 3/8”-16×3¾” security Torx anchor bolts and flat washers; see Cortech instructions. Hardware not included
  • Ganging: Bolt chairs together with 3/8”-16×2½” security Torx bolts and flat washers; see Cortech instructions. Hardware not included
  • Ballast Door: Hardware bit size is T27

Applications

The Sentinel Armless Chair suits rooms that need compact, sturdy seating that is easy to arrange.

Typical applications include:

  • Dayrooms, lounges, and waiting areas in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Dayrooms, visitation rooms, and common areas in correctional facilities and jails
  • Residential treatment centers and other group spaces
