The Rapid Extraction REX DCS is a wheeled rescue litter with a built-in Descent Control System, so one responder can bring a patient down stairwells, steep grades, and rough ground at a controlled speed. It is the REX ONE multi-purpose extraction stretcher with an integrated hand brake, and it still converts into a backboard or a patient treatment cot.

Dual brake rods slow both wheels evenly from a single hand brake lever, and a safety lock button holds the brakes on without constant pressure, so the stretcher does not roll when it is stopped. Safety connection brackets and straps give responders extra control on difficult descents, which makes the REX DCS a practical stair stretcher for high-rise evacuations, mass casualty incidents, and EMS and fire teams.

Uses

The REX DCS suits teams that need to move patients down the stairs or steep ground with fewer responders.

Typical uses include:

  • High-rise and multi-story evacuation: Bring patients down stairwells when the elevators cannot be used
  • Steep grades and rough terrain: Controlled descent on hillsides, embankments, and trails
  • EMS and fire departments: One responder can move a patient from the scene to the ambulance
  • Mass casualty incidents and medical surge: One responder per patient frees others for patient care
  • Hospitals and care facilities: Evacuate non-ambulatory patients down the stairs
  • Bike patrols and events: With the bike tow attachment, a bike team can tow the stretcher across large sites

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the descent control brake work?

Squeeze the hand brake lever to slow both wheels through the dual brake rods. Press the safety lock button to hold the brakes on without keeping pressure on the lever.

Can one responder take a patient down the stairs?

Yes. The REX DCS is designed for a single responder, and the hand brake controls the speed on the way down. The safety connection brackets and straps let a second responder add control on difficult descents.

How is the REX DCS different from the REX ONE?

The REX DCS has the same tri-fold frame, configurations, and 400 lb capacity as the REX ONE, and adds the Descent Control System brake for stairs and steep terrain.

Is the REX DCS a rescue litter?

Yes, a wheeled one. Unlike a basket litter that a team carries, the REX DCS rolls on all-terrain wheels, brakes on the stairs, and folds into a backpack case.
