The Cortech Heavy Duty PVC Corner Guards protect drywall, plaster, and concrete wall corners from chips, dings, and damage in correctional and behavioral healthcare facilities, kitchens, hallways, storage areas, and parking garages, anywhere carts, forklifts, or dollies are used.

The 48” guards are made of PVC in the USA and come in 1” to 4” widths, a 2½” bullnose for rounded or obtuse corners, a 1½” wood grain style, and 3” and 4” diamond tread. Each one mounts with factory-installed adhesive tape or with tamper-resistant screws.

  • Material: Heavy duty PVC with high impact resistance; virtually unbreakable
  • Length: 48” (8’ lengths available on request)
  • Widths: 1”, 2”, 3”, or 4” standard; 2½” bullnose; 1½” wood grain; 3” or 4” diamond tread
  • Edges: Beveled edge for a finished look
  • Bullnose: Bullnose corner guards fit rounded or obtuse corners
  • Mounting: Factory installed adhesive tape, or tamper-resistant screws (hardware included)
  • Indoor or Outdoor Use: Weather resistant for interior or exterior use
  • Flammability: Flame retardant, except clear corner guards and corner guards with adhesive mounting
  • Colors: Almond, Black, Bronze, Clear, Eggshell, Gray, Silver, White, or Yellow; wood grain in Beige, Blue, or White; diamond tread in Silver or Yellow. Clear is available with screw mounting only
  • Care: Easy to clean with a soap and water solution
  • Packaging: Price is per piece. Packed 20 per case; must buy in full cases
  • Warranty: 10-year factory warranty
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Installation and Options

  • Adhesive Mount: Factory installed adhesive tape
  • Screw Mount: Tamper-resistant screws; hardware included

Applications

Corner guards suit busy areas where wall corners take hits from carts, beds, and equipment.

Typical applications include:

  • Hallways, dayrooms, and kitchens in correctional facilities and jails
  • Hallways and common areas in behavioral healthcare facilities and hospitals
  • Storage areas, loading areas, and parking garages
