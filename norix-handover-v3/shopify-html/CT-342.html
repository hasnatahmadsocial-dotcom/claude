The Cortech Summit Side Table is a low side table with a molded base and a laminate delta top, for lounges, dayrooms, waiting areas, and patient spaces in behavioral healthcare and other secure settings. It coordinates with Summit seating for a calm, residential look.

The one-piece molded base has no seams or joints to tamper with. The table is designed to be weighted with sandbags in the base.

  • Base: One-piece rotationally molded high impact polyethylene; no steel or wood components
  • Top: High-pressure laminate top with a Dura-Edge; it resists moisture and impact and cleans easily
  • Sizes: Small delta top (342 / 342S) or medium delta top (343 / 343S)
  • Dimensions: 22”W×22”D×18”H
  • Weight: 23 lbs (top 11 lbs, base 12 lbs)
  • Weighting: The base can be filled with sandbags; do not use loose sand. Optional bolt-down plates available
  • Flammability Standards: Laminate surfaces pass CAL 117; the base is flame retardant and passes California Technical Bulletin 133
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD Gold (laminate tops not included)
  • Easy Cleaning: Sound dampening, and easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Base Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty on the base; 5-year warranty on the laminate top, against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 342 – Small Delta Top: Without ballast door
  • 342S – Small Delta Top with Ballast Door: Ballast door for adding sandbags
  • 343 – Medium Delta Top: Without ballast door
  • 343S – Medium Delta Top with Ballast Door: Ballast door for adding sandbags

Installation and Options

  • Assembly: The base attaches to the top with tamper-resistant screws; hardware bit size is T40
  • Weighting: Add sandbags through the ballast door in the base on the 342S and 343S; do not use loose sand. Ballast door bit size is T27

Applications

The Summit Side Table suits lounges and patient areas that want a small, stable table that is easy to clean.

Typical applications include:

  • Dayrooms, lounges, and waiting areas in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Dining and activity areas in residential treatment centers
  • Other secure settings that want a calm, residential look
