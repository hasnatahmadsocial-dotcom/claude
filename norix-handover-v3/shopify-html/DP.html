The Norix Double Pedestal Table is a 36”×96” laminate table on two molded polyethylene bases, with room for up to eight people to dine, work, or gather in dayrooms and dining areas of behavioral healthcare and correctional facilities. It comes with Evvolve bases or Jupiter bases.

With a base at each end, the open space under the top meets ADA accessibility requirements for wheelchairs and other mobility aids, which a single pedestal table cannot do. The bases are made to be ballasted on site with 400 lbs of dry sand (200 lbs per base), or Norix can ballast them at the factory. Ballast or a bolt-down kit is required to validate the warranty.

  • Bases: Two one-piece rotationally molded high-impact polyethylene bases with UV stabilizers, in the ribbed Evvolve style or the cylinder Jupiter style
  • Laminate Top: High-pressure laminate over a 45 lb density M3 particle board core, with a 1½” thick radiused molded edge that is pressure fused to the top
  • Top Attachment: Embedded T-nuts give the laminate top a steel-to-steel connection to the base
  • Top Size: 36”×96”
  • Height: 31.1” (Evvolve bases) or 29.3” (Jupiter bases)
  • Space Between Bases: 31” (Evvolve bases) or 35.8” (Jupiter bases)
  • Weight: 196 lbs (Evvolve bases) or 180 lbs (Jupiter bases), without ballast
  • Plain Top Finishes: Titanium Evolve, Wild Cherry, Kensington Maple, Asian Sun, Montana Walnut, Palisades Oak, Calcutta Marble, or Black Alicante laminate with a matching or black molded edge
  • Base Colors: Evvolve bases in Twilight, Cove, Dove, Sienna, or Raven, or Citrus, Tranquil, Malibu, Cobalt, Wisteria, LUX Ivory, and LUX Onyx with a 6-piece minimum per color; Jupiter bases in Raven
  • Accessibility: ADA compliant for wheelchairs and other mobility aids
  • Easy Cleaning: The bases can be cleaned with bleach solutions and hospital-grade disinfectants without affecting the surface or color
  • Static Load Testing: Tested to 1,000 lbs
  • Flammability Standards: California Technical Bulletin 133 and ASTM E1537
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD and GREENGUARD Gold
  • Warranty: 10-year limited replacement warranty
  • Country of Manufacture: USA

Available Models and Options

  • EV3696 – Evvolve Bases: 36”×96”, 31.1” high, 196 lbs
  • CY3696 – Jupiter Bases: 36”×96”, 29.3” high, 180 lbs

Installation and Options

  • Ballasting: Fill the bases on site with 400 lbs of dry sand, 200 lbs per base (not supplied), or order factory ballasting. Ballast or a bolt-down kit is required to validate the warranty.
  • Bolt-Down: Optional bolt-down kit
  • Assembly: None required

Applications

The Norix Double Pedestal Table suits dining areas and dayrooms that need one large table for a group, including a place for a wheelchair user.

Typical applications include:

  • Dayrooms and dining halls in correctional and detention facilities
  • Behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Visitation areas, classrooms, and other common areas

About Norix Dayroom and Dining Tables

Norix tables are made for dayrooms, dining halls, classrooms, and visitation areas in correctional and behavioral healthcare facilities, where furniture is used hard every day. The range covers molded-base tables such as the Jupiter, Evvolve, and Molded X-Base; Laminate X-Base tables; and steel-base tables such as the Leg Style, Multi-Purpose, Max-Master, and Oasis.

Common characteristics of Norix tables include:

  • Durable Tops: High-pressure laminate over a dense particle board core, with molded or T-mold edges depending on the model
  • Secure Connections: Tops attach to the base with tamper-resistant hardware for a solid metal-to-metal connection
  • Placement Options: Depending on the model, tables can be ballasted, bolted to the floor, or set on tamper-resistant glides
  • Easy Cleaning: Smooth surfaces that stand up to routine cleaning in high-traffic areas
  • Made in the USA: Built by Norix in the United States
