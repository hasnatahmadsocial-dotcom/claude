The Cortech Endurance Cylinder Base Molded Table with Round Top is a fully molded 42” round table for dining rooms, dayrooms, and activity rooms in correctional and behavioral healthcare facilities. The top and cylinder base are rotationally molded from high impact “no break” polyethylene, and the rounded edge of the top adds safety and comfort.

The base attaches to the top with tamper-resistant screws, and the table can be bolted to the floor through concealed pilot holes with the Cortech Sabre Mount System. An optional ballast door lets you add weight with sandbags. It comes with a plain top or a game top, pairs with the Cortech RazorBack Chair, and is rated for outdoor use.

  • Material: Top and base rotationally molded from high impact “no break” polyethylene with an antimicrobial compound
  • Base: Molded cylinder base; attaches to the top with tamper-resistant screws
  • Top Size: 42” round, with a rounded edge
  • Dimensions: 42”W×42”D×29”H
  • Weight: 38 lbs (top 22 lbs, base 16 lbs)
  • Game Top: Optional game top with a checkerboard pattern
  • Outdoor Use: Rated for outdoor use and UV stabilized
  • Antimicrobial Testing: Tested to ISO 22196, showing a greater than 99% reduction in bacteria
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133 and UL 94 HB
  • Easy Cleaning: Sound dampening, and easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Installation and Options

  • Floor Mounting: Bolt to the floor through concealed pilot holes with the Sabre Mount System
  • Ballast: Optional ballast door for adding weight with sandbags (T27 bit); Cortech says not to use loose sand
  • Assembly: The base attaches to the top with tamper-resistant screws and washers; see Cortech instructions

Applications

The Endurance Cylinder Base Table suits dining rooms, dayrooms, and activity rooms where a table has to stand up to hard daily use and frequent cleaning.

Typical applications include:

  • Dining rooms and dayrooms in correctional facilities and jails
  • Behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Residential treatment centers and activity rooms
