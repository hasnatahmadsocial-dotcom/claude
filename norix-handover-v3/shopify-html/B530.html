The Norix Ironman Stack-A-Bunk Bed Platform is a temporary sleep surface that keeps people off the floor when a facility is overcrowded or needs emergency bunking. Most courts do not allow inmates to sleep on the floor, and the Stack-A-Bunk gives facilities a washable, stackable platform that can be stored until it is needed.

Each platform is rotationally molded in one piece from high-impact polyethylene with UV stabilizers, so it has no moving parts or components that could be turned into a weapon. The seamless, non-porous surface does not absorb fluids, gives bed bugs nowhere to hide, and can be cleaned with bleach solutions and hospital-grade disinfectants. When not in use, 22 platforms stack 72” high.

  • Material: One-piece rotationally molded high-impact polyethylene with UV stabilizers and TruColor™ fully compounded color
  • Chemical Resistance: Resists blood, vinegar, urine, feces, salt solution, and chlorine solution
  • Stacking: 22 platforms stack 72” high for storage

Applications

The Stack-A-Bunk is made for temporary bunking, so facilities can keep platforms in storage and set them up when the population rises.

Typical applications include:

  • Prisons, jails, and detention centers
  • Disaster relief, emergency, mass care, night, and transitional housing shelters
  • Overcrowding and emergency temporary bunking
  • Intake, holding, and other short-term housing areas
