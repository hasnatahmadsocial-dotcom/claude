The Cortech Chuckwagon Jr. Insulated Food Tray Delivery Cart is the compact version of the Chuckwagon, for smaller housing units in prisons, jails, and other correctional facilities. It holds up to 88 Rock Trays, 68 Rock 2.0 Trays, or 64 X-Trays, and it keeps hot food hot and cold food cold on the way.

The cart has a one-piece molded cavity with no seams, no welded walls, and no adhesives, and its walls are filled with high grade insulation. The two insulated sliding doors have no hinges and lift out for cleaning. The C1716LH adds a tamper-resistant locking hasp for the doors.

  • Material: High impact polyethylene
  • Construction: One-piece molded cavity with no seams, no welded walls, and no adhesives
  • Insulation: Walls and doors are filled with high grade insulation to keep hot food hot and cold food cold
  • Dimensions: 43”L×30”W×59”H
  • Weight: 195 lbs
  • Load Capacity: 1,000 lbs
  • Tray Size: Holds any tray up to 9½”×14½”
  • Tray Capacity: 88 Rock Trays (56 with lids), 68 Rock 2.0 Trays (48 with lids), or 64 X-Trays (40 with lids); trays sold separately
  • Doors: Two one-piece insulated sliding doors with no hinges; they lift out for cleaning
  • Locking Hasp: Optional tamper-resistant locking door latch
  • Top Surface: Raised top edge safely holds more trays, beverage servers, and wash racks
  • Handles: Handle slots for easy navigation
  • Casters: Non-marring kingpin-less casters
  • Bumpers: Built-in wall bumpers
  • Color: Brown (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: 3-year factory warranty
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • C1716 – Standard: Without locking hasp
  • C1716LH – With Locking Hasp: Tamper-resistant locking door latch

Installation and Options

  • Assembly: Hardware bit size is T45

Applications

The Chuckwagon Jr. suits smaller housing units and facilities that need an insulated tray cart with a smaller footprint.

Typical applications include:

  • Meal delivery from central kitchens to housing units in prisons and jails
  • Detention centers and juvenile facilities
  • Other secure facilities that deliver meals on trays
