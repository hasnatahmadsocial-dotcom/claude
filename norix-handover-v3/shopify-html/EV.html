The Norix Evvolve Table pairs a ribbed, rotationally molded polyethylene base with a laminate or DuoForm™ top, for dayrooms, dining areas, activity rooms, and visitation areas in behavioral healthcare and correctional facilities. It comes in 36”, 42”, and 48” round and 36” and 42” square sizes, with a plain laminate top or a game top. The 42” square size is also available with a molded DuoForm™ top.

Norix designed the ribbed base around rotational symmetry, a pattern linked to a calm, comforting environment. The tapered shape adds stability when the base is ballasted and leaves room to clean around it. The base is made to be weighted on site with 200 lbs of dry sand or gravel, or Norix can ballast it at the factory. Ballast or a bolt-down kit is required to validate the warranty.

  • Base: One-piece rotationally molded high-impact polyethylene with UV stabilizers and a ribbed, tapered design
  • Laminate Top: High-pressure laminate over a 45 lb density M3 particle board core, with a 1½” thick radiused molded edge that is pressure fused to the top
  • DuoForm™ Top: Fused layers of high-impact polyethylene with UV stabilizers, in Cove or Twilight, fastened to the base with rivnuts for a steel-to-steel connection
  • Top Attachment: Embedded T-nuts give the laminate top a steel-to-steel connection to the base
  • Top Sizes: 36”, 42”, or 48” round; 36” or 42” square
  • Height: 31.1”
  • Weight: 55 to 106 lbs, depending on size and top (without ballast)
  • Plain Top Finishes: Titanium Evolve, Wild Cherry, Kensington Maple, Asian Sun, Montana Walnut, Palisades Oak, Calcutta Marble, or Black Alicante laminate with a matching or black molded edge
  • Game Top: Optional Titanium Evolve laminate with a printed game board, black molded edge
  • Base Colors: Twilight, Cove, Dove, Sienna, or Raven; Citrus, Tranquil, Malibu, Cobalt, Wisteria, LUX Ivory, and LUX Onyx with a 6-piece minimum per color
  • Easy Cleaning: The base resists blood, vinegar, urine, feces, salt solution, and chlorine solution, and can be cleaned with bleach solutions and hospital-grade disinfectants
  • Static Load Testing: Tested to 1,000 lbs
  • Flammability Standards: California Technical Bulletin 133 and ASTM E1537
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD and GREENGUARD Gold
  • Warranty: 10-year limited replacement warranty (5 years for the DuoForm™ top)
  • Intended Use: Indoor use only
  • Country of Manufacture: USA

  • Ballasting: Fill the base on site with 200 lbs of dry sand or gravel (not supplied), or order factory ballasting. Ballast or a bolt-down kit is required to validate the warranty.
  • Bolt-Down: Optional bolt-down kit
  • Concealment Plate: Optional for the DuoForm™ model
  • Assembly: Some assembly required; see Norix instructions

About Norix Dayroom and Dining Tables

Norix tables are made for dayrooms, dining halls, classrooms, and visitation areas in correctional and behavioral healthcare facilities, where furniture is used hard every day. The range covers molded-base tables such as the Jupiter, Evvolve, and Molded X-Base; Laminate X-Base tables; and steel-base tables such as the Leg Style, Multi-Purpose, Max-Master, and Oasis.

Common characteristics of Norix tables include:

  • Durable Tops: High-pressure laminate over a dense particle board core, with molded or T-mold edges depending on the model
  • Secure Connections: Tops attach to the base with tamper-resistant hardware for a solid metal-to-metal connection
  • Placement Options: Depending on the model, tables can be ballasted, bolted to the floor, or set on tamper-resistant glides
  • Easy Cleaning: Smooth surfaces that stand up to routine cleaning in high-traffic areas
  • Made in the USA: Built by Norix in the United States
