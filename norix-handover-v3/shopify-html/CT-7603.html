The Cortech Endurance Wall Mount Desk is a one-piece molded desk that bolts to the wall and floor, for cells, patient rooms, residential units, and other secure settings. Choose the 7603 with a smooth laminate work surface for a more normative look, or the all-plastic 7605.

The desk is rotationally molded from high impact “no break” polyethylene, with no steel components, and bolts in place through concealed pilot holes with hardware of your choice.

  • Material: One-piece rotationally molded high impact “no break” polyethylene; no steel components
  • Top: Smooth laminate top in a choice of standard Formica and Wilsonart finishes (7603), or all-plastic molded top (7605)
  • Dimensions: 36”W×24”D×29”H
  • Weight: 35 lbs
  • Mounting: Bolts to the floor and wall through concealed pilot holes
  • Antimicrobial Testing: Tested to ISO 22196, showing a greater than 99% reduction in bacteria
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133 and UL 94 HB
  • Easy Cleaning: Sound dampening, and easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty on the molded desk; 5-year warranty on the laminate top (7603)
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 7603 – Laminate Top: 36”W×24”D×29”H, 35 lbs
  • 7605 – All Plastic: 36”W×24”D×29”H, 35 lbs

Installation and Options

  • Mounting: Bolt to the floor and wall through concealed pilot holes with hardware of your choice; drill holes at least 2” deep; see Cortech instructions

Applications

The Endurance Wall Mount Desk suits rooms where a desk must stay fixed in place and stand up to hard use, with a laminate top where a warmer, more normative surface is wanted.

Typical applications include:

  • Cells and housing units in correctional facilities and jails
  • Patient rooms in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Residential treatment centers, classrooms, and study areas
