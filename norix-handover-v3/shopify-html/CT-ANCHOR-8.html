The Cortech Anchor 8-Seat Rectangle Laminate Table with Molded Seats is a fixed group seating table for dayrooms and dining areas in correctional and behavioral healthcare facilities. The 30”×96” laminate top and eight molded seats are joined to a continuous welded steel frame that bolts to the floor, so the table and seats stay in place.

The seats are rotationally molded from high impact “no break” polyethylene and attach to the frame through molded-in threaded steel inserts and tamper-resistant hardware. The seamless laminate top has a rounded edge for safety and comfort.

  • Seats: One-piece rotationally molded high impact “no break” polyethylene, with molded-in threaded steel inserts for a tamper-resistant, steel-to-steel attachment to the frame
  • Top: Seamless laminate top with high impact resistance and a rounded edge for safety and comfort; attaches to the frame with tamper-resistant screws
  • Frame: Continuous seam welded 3” diameter, 14-gauge powder coated steel with 11-gauge steel seat supports
  • Legs: J-leg (Anchor) or U-leg (Anchor 2) frame
  • Seating: 8 seats
  • Top Size: 30”×96” rectangle
  • Weight: 265 lbs
  • ADA Version: Made with one fewer leg and seat (7 seats) for wheelchair access; 240 lbs
  • Top Color: Gray (custom colors available on request)
  • Seat Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Easy Cleaning: Easy to clean and maintain
  • Warranty: 3-year warranty on the steel legs (must be bolted down); 5-year warranty on the laminate top
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 3096ADPB – 30”×96” Rectangle Top: 8 seats, 265 lbs

Installation and Options

  • Floor Mounting: Must be bolted to the floor; drill holes at least 2” deep and use concrete anchors
  • Assembly: Required; the top and seats attach to the frame with tamper-resistant screws (T40 bit); see Cortech instructions

Applications

The Anchor 8-Seat Rectangle Table suits dayrooms and dining areas where tables and seats must stay fixed in place and stand up to continuous use.

Typical applications include:

  • Correctional dayrooms and dining halls
  • Jails and detention centers
  • Behavioral healthcare facilities and other secure group areas

About Cortech Anchor Tables

Cortech Anchor Tables are fixed group seating tables for dayrooms and dining areas in correctional and behavioral healthcare facilities. Each table has a laminate top and rotationally molded seats on a continuous welded steel frame that bolts to the floor.

Anchor Tables come in four sizes:

  • 4-Seat Table: 42” round or 42”×42” square top
  • 6-Seat Round Table: 48” round top
  • 6-Seat Rectangle Table: 30”×72” top
  • 8-Seat Rectangle Table: 30”×96” top

Every size can be made with one fewer leg and seat for ADA wheelchair access, and the frame comes with J-legs (Anchor) or U-legs (Anchor 2).
