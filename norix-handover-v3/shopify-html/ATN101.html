The Norix Attenda Anti-Ligature Platform Bed is the core piece of the Attenda Series, designed to be ligature resistant for resident rooms in behavioral healthcare and correctional facilities. It can be mounted in a corner or against a wall, or used free-standing, and a raised lip around the bed deck makes cleanup easier.

The bed is rotationally molded from high-impact polyethylene and filled with rigid polyurethane foam for strength and sound absorption. It is tested to a 1,000 lb static load and fits a 38” × 82” mattress. Four versions cover floor-mounted and free-standing installations, with or without mounting holes.

  • Construction: Rotationally molded high-impact polyethylene with UV stabilizers, filled with rigid polyurethane foam
  • Design: Seamless molded shape with no internal openings and a raised lip around the bed deck
  • Overall Dimensions: 84” L × 40” W × 15.5” H
  • Bed Deck: 82” L × 38” W
  • Mattress Size: 38” × 82”
  • Weight: 108 to 115 lbs, depending on model
  • Colors: Twilight, Cove, Sand, Sienna, Dove
  • Static Load Testing: Tested to 1,000 lbs
  • Flammability Standards: California Technical Bulletin 133 and 117, UL 1056, ASTM E1537, and NFPA 261
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD and GREENGUARD Gold
  • Warranty: 10-year limited replacement warranty
  • Intended Use: Indoor use only
  • Country of Manufacture: USA

Available Models and Options

  • ATN101 – Floor-Mount Bed: Mounting holes, 110 lbs
  • ATN101B – Floor-Mount Bed with Bottom Closure: Mounting holes and fully enclosed bottom, 115 lbs
  • ATN103 – Free-Standing Bed: No mounting holes, 110 lbs
  • ATN103B – Free-Standing Bed with Bottom Closure: No mounting holes, fully enclosed bottom, 108 lbs

Installation and Options

  • Floor-Mounted Installation: Wedge anchors and 3/8” bolts are required and are not supplied by Norix. A groove along the underside edge accepts security caulk (caulk not supplied).
  • Accessories: Removable restraint ring kits (set of 6 or single), enclosure kit (ATN106), bed riser (ATN102-K) that raises the sleep surface 8” for easier care and patient lift use, wall-mount headboard (ATN110), and glides (ATN-G103)
  • Assembly: None required

Applications

The Norix Attenda Platform Bed gives residents a durable, easy-to-clean bed in rooms where ligature resistance and safety are important considerations.

Typical applications include:

  • Behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Correctional and detention facilities
  • Other supervised residential and institutional settings

About Norix Attenda Molded Bedroom Series

The Norix Attenda Series is a line of molded bedroom furniture for behavioral healthcare and correctional facilities. It includes beds, nightstands, open chests, desks, a stool, wall-mounted storage units, and a wardrobe, all molded from high-impact polyethylene in a clean, contemporary style. Each piece is designed to attach flush to the wall or floor.

Common characteristics of the Attenda Series include:

  • Molded Construction: Rotationally molded high-impact polyethylene with smooth, rounded edges
  • Ligature-Resistant Design: Molded shapes without pass-throughs or tie-off points to help reduce ligature risk
  • Contraband-Resistant Design: No creases or nooks, which helps reduce places to hide contraband
  • Easy Cleaning: Chemically resistant surfaces that can be cleaned with bleach solutions and hospital-grade disinfectants
  • TruColor™ Technology: Fully compounded color for a consistent finish without streaks or speckles
