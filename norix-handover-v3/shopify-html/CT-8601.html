The Cortech Endurance Workstation is a space-saving desk for compact cells, patient rooms, and other secure settings. It mounts directly to the wall and can be paired with Endurance Workstation Seats, used with other seating, or used as a shelf. The desk is rotationally molded in one piece from high impact “no break” polyethylene, with no steel or wood components.

  • Material: One-piece rotationally molded high impact “no break” polyethylene; no steel or wood components
  • Use: Pairs with Endurance Workstation Seats (8602) or other seating, or can be used as a shelf
  • Dimensions: 30”W×18”D×11”H
  • Weight: 35 lbs
  • Load Testing: 2,000 lb static load tested
  • Mounting: Bolts to the wall through concealed pilot holes
  • Outdoor Use: Rated for outdoor use and UV stabilized
  • Antimicrobial Testing: Tested to ISO 22196, showing a greater than 99% reduction in bacteria
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133 and UL 94 HB
  • Easy Cleaning: Sound dampening, and easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 8601 – Workstation Desk: 30”W×18”D×11”H, 35 lbs; seats sold separately

Installation and Options

  • Wall Mounting: Drill through the six pilot holes, mark the wall, and bolt the desk to the wall with six 3/8”-16×3¾” security Torx anchor bolts and flat washers in holes at least 2” deep; see Cortech instructions. Hardware not included
  • Can be paired with Endurance Workstation Seats (as shown) or with any other seat

Applications

The Workstation Desk suits small rooms where a desk must be fixed to the wall and floor space is limited.

Typical applications include:

  • Cells and housing units in correctional facilities and jails
  • Patient rooms in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Compact rooms and study areas where floor space is limited

About Cortech Endurance Series

The Cortech Endurance Series is a line of heavy-duty molded furniture for behavioral healthcare facilities, correctional institutions, and other intense use environments. Endurance products are made for durability, easy cleaning, and safety-conscious design.

The Endurance Series includes:
