The Cortech Endurance Nightstand is a one-piece molded bedside night table with molded-in shelving for open storage of personal items. It gives patient rooms and housing units a durable nightstand with 2 cu. ft. of storage and no doors or drawers.

The nightstand is rotationally molded from high impact “no break” polyethylene, with no steel or wood components. It can be bolted to the floor or wall through concealed pilot holes, and it comes with a plain molded top (7602) or a smooth laminate top (7609).

  • Material: One-piece rotationally molded high impact “no break” polyethylene; no steel or wood components
  • Storage: Molded-in open shelving, 2 cu. ft.
  • Dimensions: 21”W×18”D×22”H
  • Weight: 26 lbs
  • Top: Plain molded top (7602) or optional smooth laminate top (7609)
  • Mounting: Bolts to the floor or wall through concealed pilot holes
  • Antimicrobial Testing: Tested to ISO 22196, showing a greater than 99% reduction in bacteria
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133 and UL 94 HB
  • Easy Cleaning: Sound dampening, and easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Base Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty on the all-plastic model; 5-year warranty on the laminate top
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 7602 – Plain Top: 21”W×18”D×22”H, 26 lbs
  • 7609 – Laminate Top: 21”W×18”D×22”H, smooth laminate top, 26 lbs

Installation and Options

  • Mounting: Bolt to the floor and wall through eight concealed mounting points; drill holes at least 2” deep; see Cortech instructions. Hardware not included
  • Ballast: Optional sand ballasting

Applications

The Nightstand suits patient rooms and housing units that need a safe bedside table with open storage.

Typical applications include:

  • Patient rooms in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Cells and housing units in correctional facilities and jails
  • Residential treatment centers and shelters
