The Cortech Endurance All Purpose Wall Mount Shelf / Step is a small molded shelf that bolts to the wall in cells and patient rooms. It can hold a TV, books, or small belongings, or be installed as a step for climbing onto an upper Endurance Wall Bunk.

The shelf is rotationally molded in one piece from high impact “no break” polyethylene, with no steel or wood components. It comes in two sizes: the 7600 and the larger 8600 Shelf 2.0. Installed with the angled side facing up, it gives a steadier foot placement as a step and helps reduce ligature risk.

  • Material: One-piece rotationally molded high impact “no break” polyethylene; no steel or wood components
  • Shelf or Step: As a shelf, install with the angled side facing down; as a step, or to reduce ligature risk, turn it 180° so the angled side faces up
  • Dimensions: 15”W×6”D×5”H (7600) or 16”W×10”D×8”H (8600)
  • Weight: 2½ lbs (7600) or 4 lbs (8600)
  • Mounting: Bolts to the wall through concealed pilot holes
  • Antimicrobial Testing: Tested to ISO 22196, showing a greater than 99% reduction in bacteria
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133 and UL 94 HB
  • Easy Cleaning: Sound dampening, and easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 7600 – All Purpose Shelf: 15”W×6”D×5”H, 2½ lbs
  • 8600 – All Purpose Shelf 2.0: Larger size, 16”W×10”D×8”H, 4 lbs

Installation and Options

  • Wall Mounting: Bolt to the wall through concealed pilot holes; Cortech instructions show three (7600) or four (8600) 3/8”-16×2½” tamper-proof security Torx anchor bolts. Hardware not included
  • Direction: Angled side down for use as a shelf; angled side up for use as a step or to reduce ligature risk

Applications

The All Purpose Shelf / Step suits rooms that need a small fixed shelf, or a safe step up to an upper wall bunk.

Typical applications include:

  • Cells and housing units in correctional facilities and jails
  • Patient rooms in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Rooms with Endurance Wall Bunks, as a step to the upper bunk
