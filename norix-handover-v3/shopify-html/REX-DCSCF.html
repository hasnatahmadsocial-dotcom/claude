The Rapid Extraction REX DCS/CF is a correctional facility stretcher for jail and prison emergency response teams. It combines a folding rescue stretcher with built-in restraints and a descent control brake, so one officer can move an injured, ill, or restrained inmate across the facility, down the stairs, or out of the building in an emergency.

The tri-fold aluminum frame stores in a small space, is ready to roll in under 30 seconds, and has no components that can become weapons. Humane Restraint ankle and wrist cuffs attach easily to the frame, so staff can move a restrained person safely and humanely with fewer officers.

Uses in Correctional Facilities

The REX DCS/CF suits jails, prisons, and detention centers that need to move inmates safely with fewer staff, whether the person is injured, ill, or resisting.

Typical uses include:

  • Cell extraction: Restrain and move an inmate out of a cell and through the facility
  • Man-down incidents: Move injured inmates or officers to a point of care, from recreation injuries to mass-casualty incidents
  • Infirmary and medical units: Simple, low-profile patient loading and transport within the infirmary
  • Emergency evacuation: Bring inmates down the stairs of multi-level buildings during a fire or other emergency
  • CERT and SORT teams: One officer can do work that would otherwise take several, freeing staff for other duties

Frequently Asked Questions

Can one officer move a restrained inmate on the REX DCS/CF?

Yes. Once the inmate is restrained on the stretcher, a single officer can roll the REX DCS/CF through the facility and, with the descent control brake, down the stairs.

How is the REX DCS/CF different from the REX DCS?

The REX DCS/CF adds Humane Restraint ankle and wrist cuffs, for use in correctional and detention facilities.

Can it be left with an inmate on board?

The parking brake keeps a restrained inmate from moving the stretcher by shifting their body weight. Follow your facility's policy for observing restrained inmates.
