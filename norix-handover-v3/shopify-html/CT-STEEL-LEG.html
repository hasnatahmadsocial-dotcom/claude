The Cortech Endurance Steel Leg Laminate Table is a heavy-duty table for dayrooms and dining areas in correctional and behavioral healthcare facilities. Its 4” diameter, 14-gauge tubular steel legs are fully welded to ¼” steel mounting plates and fasten to the top with tamper-resistant screws and a metal-to-metal connection.

The seamless laminate top resists impact and has a rounded bull nose Dura-Edge for safety and comfort. Tops come in 36” and 42” square, 42” and 48” round, and 30”×72” and 30”×96” rectangle, with a game top available on the square and 48” round tables. The table can be bolted to the floor or set on floor glides.

  • Top: Seamless laminate top with high impact resistance and a rounded bull nose Dura-Edge
  • Legs: 4” diameter, 14-gauge tubular steel legs, fully welded to ¼” steel mounting plates
  • Connection: The legs fasten to the top with tamper-resistant screws and a metal-to-metal connection
  • Leg bottom: Your choice of G (Floor Glide) or B (Bolt Down)
  • Top Sizes: 36”×36” or 42”×42” square, 42” or 48” round, or 30”×72” or 30”×96” rectangle
  • Height: 29”
  • Game Top: Optional on the 36” and 42” square and the 48” round tables
  • Color: Gray top
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133
  • Easy Cleaning: Sound dampening, and easy to clean and maintain
  • Warranty: 3-year warranty on the steel legs; 5-year warranty on the laminate top
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Installation and Options

  • Floor Mounting: Bolt to the floor with the bolt-down brackets (anchors not included), or use floor glides; floor bolting is recommended
  • Assembly: The legs attach to the top with tamper-resistant screws (T40 bit); see Cortech instructions

Applications

The Steel Leg Laminate Table suits dayrooms and dining areas that need a sturdy laminate table in round, square, or rectangle sizes.

Typical applications include:

  • Dining rooms and dayrooms in correctional facilities and jails
  • Behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Residential treatment centers and activity rooms
