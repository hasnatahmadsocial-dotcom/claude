The Cortech EZ Bunk Stackable Platform Bed Sleep Surface is a temporary bed for jails, prisons, and shelters that need extra sleeping space during overcrowding or emergencies. It is rotationally molded in one piece from high density polyethylene, with no seams or holes, and 20 bunks stack in 7 vertical feet when they are not in use.

Molded handles run along the sides and ends for carrying and stacking, and internal molded structural cones give the sleep surface strength and support. The EZ Bunk fits under most wall-mounted bunks and is designed for a 30”×75” mattress, sold separately.

  • Material: One-piece rotationally molded high density polyethylene with structural and flame retardant additives
  • Dimensions: 81”L×36”W×8”H
  • Sleep Surface: 30”×75” on the primary side; the other side gives a 25”×75” secondary surface
  • Mattress: Designed for a 30”×75” mattress, 4½” thick recommended (sold separately)
  • Weight: 39 lbs
  • Stacking: 20 bunks stack in 7 vertical feet
  • Handles: Integral full-length molded handles on the sides and ends
  • Antimicrobial Testing: Tested to ISO 22196, showing a greater than 99% reduction in bacteria
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133 and UL 94 HB
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD Gold
  • Easy Cleaning: Sound dampening, and easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Colors: Blue Gray (7501) or Slate Blue (7502); custom colors available on request
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Applications

The EZ Bunk is made for temporary bunking, so facilities can keep bunks stacked in storage and set them up when the population rises.

Typical applications include:

  • Prisons, jails, and detention centers
  • Homeless and emergency shelters
  • Overcrowding and emergency temporary bunking
  • Intake, holding, and other short-term housing areas
