The Cortech Cobra Can Trash Receptacle is a virtually unbreakable trash can for correctional facilities, behavioral healthcare facilities, kitchens, and other high-abuse settings. It comes in nine sizes, in either Flame Retardant or Food Grade Material for food service or general applications. The can is rotationally molded from structural polyethylene for strength with some flexibility. Molded-in handles run around the entire can and its underside for easier lifting, and molded-in floor glides reduce floor scratching when the can is dragged.

  • Material: Rotationally molded structural polyethylene; 0.22” nominal wall thickness
  • Sizes: 20, 22, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50, or 55 gallons
  • Material Options: Flame retardant or food grade and general use
  • Handles: 360° molded-in continuous handles around the entire can perimeter and underside
  • Floor Glides: Molded-in floor glides reduce floor scratching when the can is dragged
  • Durability: Virtually unbreakable
  • Flame Retardant Version: Passes California Technical Bulletin 133 and UL 94 HB; tested to ISO 22196, showing a greater than 99% reduction in bacteria
  • Colors: Flame retardant in Blue Gray (also available in Green or Blue); food grade and general use in Blue, Green, Red, White, Yellow, or Black (custom colors available on request)
  • Dolly: Optional Cobra Can dolly, sold separately
  • Warranty: 10-year factory warranty
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Applications

The Cobra Can suits secure facilities and kitchens that need a trash can that will not break, with a flame retardant version for areas that must meet flammability standards.

Typical applications include:

  • Housing units, dayrooms, and common areas in correctional facilities and jails
  • Kitchens, dining halls, and food service areas
  • Behavioral healthcare facilities and other high-abuse settings
