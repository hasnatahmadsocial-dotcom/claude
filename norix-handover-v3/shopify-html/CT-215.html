The Cortech Sequoia Molded Wood Grain Nightstand is a one-piece molded nightstand with a wood grain finish, for patient rooms in behavioral healthcare facilities and other secure settings. It matches the Sequoia Bed for a warm, residential room.

The nightstand is rotationally molded as a single unit from high impact polyethylene, with no metal or wood components, and its rounded edges and sloped surfaces help reduce ligature risk.

  • Material: One-piece rotationally molded high impact polyethylene; no metal or wood components
  • Dimensions: 18”W×16”D×21”H
  • Weight: 26 lbs
  • Safety: Rounded edges and sloped surfaces help reduce ligature risk
  • Floor Mounting: Bolts to the floor
  • Finishes: Heritage Oak, Natural Oak, Coastal Driftwood, Walnut, or Redwood wood grain (custom wood grain finishes available on request)
  • Wood Grain: Like natural wood, each piece varies slightly in color and grain
  • Antimicrobial: Infused with CORx antimicrobial compound
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133
  • Award: The Sequoia Series earned a 2025 Gold Nightingale Award in the Anti-Ligature and Reduced-Ligature Furnishings category
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 215 – Sequoia Nightstand: Heritage Oak, Natural Oak, Coastal Driftwood, Walnut, or Redwood

Installation and Options

  • Floor Mounting: Bolt to the floor through four holes drilled through the nightstand; see Cortech instructions

Applications

The Sequoia Nightstand suits patient rooms that want a warm, residential look to match the Sequoia Bed.

Typical applications include:

  • Patient rooms in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Residential treatment centers and secure treatment programs
  • Other secure settings that want a residential bedroom
