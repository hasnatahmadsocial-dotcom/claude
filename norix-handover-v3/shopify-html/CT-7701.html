The Cortech Endurance Wall Mount Bunk is a one-piece molded bed that bolts to the wall, keeping the floor clear in cells and patient rooms. Two bunks can be mounted one above the other for double bunking, and the bunk takes a standard 30”×75” mattress.

The bunk is rotationally molded from high impact “no break” polyethylene, with no steel or wood components. Molded safety rails and a molded ladder are available.

  • Material: One-piece rotationally molded high impact “no break” polyethylene; no steel or wood components
  • Dimensions: 81”W×35”D×18”H
  • Weight: 150 lbs
  • Mattress: Designed for a 30”×75” mattress; a 4½” thick mattress is recommended
  • Load Testing: 250 lb load cycled 70,000 times; 2,000 lb static load for 24 hours
  • Double Bunking: Space-saving design for double bunk use
  • Mounting: Bolts to the wall through concealed pilot holes
  • Antimicrobial Testing: Tested to ISO 22196, showing a greater than 99% reduction in bacteria
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133 and UL 94 HB
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD Gold
  • Easy Cleaning: Sound dampening, easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain, and resistant to stains
  • Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 7701 – Wall Mount Bunk: 81”W×35”D×18”H, 150 lbs
  • 7702 – Safety Rail, 61.5”: 61½”L×4”W×12”H, 14 lbs; smooth, curved edges for ligature resistance
  • 7703 – Small Safety Rail, 42”: 42”L×4”W×12”H, 9 lbs; smooth, curved edges for ligature resistance
  • 7704 – Wall Bunk Ladder: 15½”L×4½”W×55”H, 34 lbs; smooth, curved surfaces with no pass-throughs for ligature resistance

Installation and Options

  • Wall Mounting: Bolt the bunk to the wall through concealed pilot holes. Hardware not included
  • Safety Rails and Ladder: Bolt to the Wall Bunk with the tamper-resistant hardware provided; see Cortech instructions
  • Ladder Spacing: For ladder use, mount the two bunks 39” to 44” apart
  • Step Option: Cortech also offers the Endurance All Purpose Shelf, which can be installed as a step to the upper bunk

Applications

The Endurance Wall Mount Bunk suits rooms where beds must be fixed to the wall and floor space is limited.

Typical applications include:

  • Cells and housing units in correctional facilities and jails
  • Patient rooms in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Double-bunked rooms in detention centers and other secure facilities
