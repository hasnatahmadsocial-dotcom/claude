The Cortech Endurance ADA Wall Mount Desk gives people who use wheelchairs a fixed, accessible desk in cells, patient rooms, and other secure settings. It meets all ADA requirements, with knee and toe space and clear floor space for a front approach. The desk is rotationally molded in one piece from high impact “no break” polyethylene, with no steel or wood components.

  • Material: One-piece rotationally molded high impact “no break” polyethylene; no steel or wood components
  • ADA Compliance: Meets all ADA requirements, with knee and toe space and clear floor space for a front approach
  • Dimensions: 38”W×22”D×31”H
  • Weight: 34 lbs
  • Mounting: Bolts to the wall and floor through concealed pilot holes
  • Antimicrobial Testing: Tested to ISO 22196, showing a greater than 99% reduction in bacteria
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133 and UL 94 HB
  • Easy Cleaning: Sound dampening, and easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Applications

The ADA Wall Mount Desk suits accessible rooms where a desk must stay fixed in place and leave clear space for a wheelchair.

Typical applications include:

  • Accessible cells and housing units in correctional facilities and jails
  • Accessible patient rooms in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Residential treatment centers and other rooms that need an ADA-compliant desk
