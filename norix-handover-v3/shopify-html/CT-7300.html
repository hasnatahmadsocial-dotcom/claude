The Cortech Endurance Locker is a full-height molded property storage locker for patient rooms, housing units, and other secure settings. It has 7.75 cu. ft. of storage on two fixed internal shelves, an anti-ligature shelf for hanging clothes, and a top shelf for additional storage.

The locker is rotationally molded from high impact “no break” polyethylene, with no wood components. The 7300 has a door with a flush-mounted Master Lock combination lock and center key override, and the door hinges are molded plastic, with no metal hinges. The 7200 is the same locker without a door. Both bolt to the wall.

  • Material: Rotationally molded high impact “no break” polyethylene; no wood components
  • Storage: 7.75 cu. ft. of internal shelf storage, with two non-removable shelves, an anti-ligature shelf for hanging clothes, and a top shelf for additional storage
  • Interior: Slightly sloped for easy washing and drainage
  • Lock (7300): Flush-mounted Master Lock combination lock with center key override; master keys available on request so staff can override the combination
  • Hinges (7300): Molded plastic interior hinging system, with no metal hinges
  • Dimensions: 26”W×26”D×78½”H
  • Mounting: Bolts to the wall through concealed pilot holes
  • Antimicrobial Testing: Tested to ISO 22196, showing a greater than 99% reduction in bacteria
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133 and UL 94 HB
  • Easy Cleaning: Sound dampening, and easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 7300 – Locker with Door: Combination lock with center key override
  • 7200 – Locker without Door: Open locker with no door or lock

Installation and Options

  • Wall Mounting: Drill the ten mounting points on the back of the locker, mark the wall, and bolt the locker to the wall with ten security Torx anchor bolts and flat washers in holes at least 2” deep; see Cortech instructions. Hardware not included

Applications

The Endurance Locker suits rooms where residents need secure, private storage for clothing and personal property.

Typical applications include:

  • Patient rooms in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Housing units and dormitories in correctional facilities and jails
  • Residential treatment centers and shelters
