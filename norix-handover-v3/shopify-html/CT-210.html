The Cortech Sequoia Molded Wood Grain Bed is a one-piece molded platform bed with a wood grain finish, for patient rooms in behavioral healthcare facilities and other secure settings. It brings the warmth of a residential wood bed with the strength and cleanability of molded polyethylene.

The bed is rotationally molded as a single unit from high impact “no break” polyethylene, and its rounded edges and sloped surfaces help reduce ligature risk. It can be bolted to the floor or weighted with sandbags through optional underside ballast doors.

  • Material: One-piece rotationally molded high impact “no break” polyethylene; no steel or wood components
  • Dimensions: 82”L×42”W×18”H
  • Weight: 126 lbs
  • Mattress: Fits a 36”×80” mattress up to 6” thick (sold separately)
  • Safety: Rounded edges and sloped surfaces help reduce ligature risk
  • Storage: No shelves or storage
  • Ballast Doors: Optional underside ballast doors for adding sandbags, secured with tamper-resistant screws; do not use loose sand
  • Floor Mounting: Bolts to the floor with a concealed corner mounting system
  • Finishes: Heritage Oak, Natural Oak, Coastal Driftwood, Walnut, or Redwood wood grain (custom wood grain finishes available on request)
  • Wood Grain: Like natural wood, each piece varies slightly in color and grain
  • Antimicrobial Testing: Tested to ISO 22196, showing a greater than 99% reduction in bacteria
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133
  • Award: The Sequoia Series earned a 2025 Gold Nightingale Award in the Anti-Ligature and Reduced-Ligature Furnishings category
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 210 – Standard: Without ballast doors
  • 210S2 – With Ballast Doors: Underside ballast doors for adding sandbags

Installation and Options

  • Floor Mounting: Mounting brackets, concrete anchors, and security Torx bolts are included; see Cortech instructions
  • Ballast Doors: Add sandbags through the underside ballast doors on the 210S2; do not use loose sand. Hardware bit size is T27

Applications

The Sequoia Bed suits patient rooms that want a warm, residential look without giving up safety or durability.

Typical applications include:

  • Patient rooms in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Residential treatment centers and secure treatment programs
  • Other secure settings that want a residential bedroom
