The Cortech Endurance Cube All Purpose End Table is a low, square molded table for dayrooms, lounges, and shared spaces in behavioral healthcare, correctional, and other secure facilities. It works as an end table, side surface, or filler piece between molded chairs.

The Cube is rotationally molded in one piece from high impact “no break” polyethylene, with no steel or wood components and a raised edge for easy clean-up. It can be bolted to the floor through concealed pilot holes, and the 126484S has underside ballast doors for adding weight with sandbags. The Cube is rated for outdoor use.

  • Material: One-piece rotationally molded high impact “no break” polyethylene; no steel or wood components
  • Top: Raised edge for easy clean-up
  • Dimensions: 24”W×24”D×15”H
  • Weight: 56 lbs
  • Ballast: The 126484S has underside ballast doors for adding weight with sandbags, secured with tamper-resistant screws
  • Outdoor Use: Rated for outdoor use and UV stabilized
  • Antimicrobial Testing: Tested to ISO 22196, showing a greater than 99% reduction in bacteria
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133 and UL 94 HB
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD Gold
  • Easy Cleaning: Sound dampening, and easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 126484 – Standard: Without access door, 56 lbs
  • 126484S – With Access Door: Underside ballast doors for adding sandbags, 56 lbs

Installation and Options

  • Floor Mounting: Bolt to the floor through concealed pilot holes. Hardware not included
  • Ballast: Add sandbags through the 126484S ballast doors; do not use loose sand

Applications

The Cube suits dayrooms and lounges that need a durable end table or filler piece between molded chairs.

Typical applications include:

  • Dayrooms and lounges in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Dayrooms and common areas in correctional facilities and jails
  • Waiting rooms and residential treatment centers
