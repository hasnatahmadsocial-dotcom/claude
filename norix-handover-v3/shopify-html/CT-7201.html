The Cortech Endurance Open Storage Chest gives residents a safe place to store and display personal belongings in patient rooms, cells, and housing units. Molded-in shelving provides 6.7 cu. ft. of open storage, with no doors or drawers.

The chest is rotationally molded in one piece from high impact “no break” polyethylene, with no steel or wood components. It can be bolted to the floor or wall through concealed pilot holes with hardware of your choice.

  • Material: One-piece rotationally molded high impact “no break” polyethylene; no steel or wood components
  • Storage: Molded-in open shelving, 6.7 cu. ft.
  • Safety: Ligature resistant design
  • Dimensions: 30”W×18”D×42”H
  • Weight: 58 lbs
  • Mounting: Bolts to the floor or wall through concealed pilot holes
  • Antimicrobial Testing: Tested to ISO 22196, showing a greater than 99% reduction in bacteria

  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD Gold

  • Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)

Applications

The Open Storage Chest suits rooms where residents need durable open storage that is easy to clean.

Typical applications include:

  • Patient rooms in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Cells and housing units in correctional facilities and jails
  • Residential treatment centers and shelters
