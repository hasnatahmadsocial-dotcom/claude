The Cortech Master Box Lockable Inmate Property Storage Box has a built-in combination dial lock, so facilities can lock inmate property without handing out padlocks. A master override key lets staff open any box when they need to, and each Master Box is individually numbered for inmate ID and property control.

The Master Box is molded from high impact “no break” polypropylene in the same three sizes as the Cortech Barracuda Box. Rounded corners and edges and vertical structural columns help it resist impact, and boxes stack with or without a lid and nest when empty. Lids are sold separately.

  • Material: High impact “no break” polypropylene
  • Lock: Built-in tamper-resistant combination dial lock with a master override key, so no padlocks are needed
  • Inmate ID: Each box is individually numbered for inmate ID and property control
  • Construction: Rounded corners and edges and vertical structural columns for strength and impact resistance
  • Stacking: Stacks with or without a lid; empty boxes nest
  • Capacity: 1.5, 2.0, or 3.0 cu ft
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133
  • Color: Gray (custom colors available on request)
  • Lid: Sold separately as the Cortech Master Box Lid
  • Easy Cleaning: Easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Warranty: 10-year warranty on the box; 5-year warranty on the lock
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Applications

The Master Box is made for facilities that want lockable property storage without the risks that come with padlocks.

Typical applications include:

  • Prisons, jails, and detention centers
  • Allowable in-cell property storage
  • Inmate records and personal property storage

About Cortech Property Storage Boxes

Cortech property storage boxes give jails, prisons, and other secure facilities a durable way to store inmate property and records. Both box lines are molded from high impact “no break” polypropylene in the same three sizes.

Cortech property storage includes:

  • Barracuda Box: Stack-and-nest property boxes in 1.5, 2.0, and 3.0 cu ft sizes, with a locking hasp on both sides
  • Master Box: Lockable property boxes in the same three sizes, with a built-in combination dial lock and master override key
  • Lids: Matching Barracuda Box and Master Box lids in two sizes, sold separately
