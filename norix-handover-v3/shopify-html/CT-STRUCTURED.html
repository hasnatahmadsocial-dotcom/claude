The Cortech Structured Seating Connected Beam Seating System is a beam-mounted seating system for waiting rooms, lobbies, dayrooms, and other common areas in correctional, behavioral healthcare, and public facilities. Each seat is rotationally molded in one piece from high impact “no break” polyethylene, with an ergonomic shape for comfort.

The seats attach to a continuous seam welded steel frame with tamper-resistant hardware through molded-in threaded steel inserts. Choose 1 to 4 seats, without arms, with arms at the ends, or with arms between every seat, and set the frame on tamper-resistant glides or bolt it to the floor.

  • Seats: One-piece rotationally molded high impact “no break” polyethylene with an antimicrobial compound
  • Seat Attachment: Molded-in threaded steel inserts give a tamper-resistant, steel-to-steel attachment to the frame
  • Frame: Continuous seam welded 2” diameter, 14-gauge powder coated steel with 11-gauge steel seat supports
  • Configurations: 1, 2, 3, or 4 seats
  • Arms: No arms, arms at the ends, or arms between every seat
  • Tables: Optional table attachments, with variable table placement
  • Height: 36”
  • Static Load: 2,000 lbs
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD Gold
  • Easy Cleaning: Easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: 3-year warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Installation and Options

  • Floor Glides or Bolt-Down: Frames come with tamper-resistant, nylon-bottom floor glides or a bolt-to-floor option
  • Floor Glide Installation: Each glide is anchored with a ¼”-16×3¾” anchor bolt, flat washer, and security Torx set screw (included), in a hole at least 2” deep; see Cortech instructions
  • Hardware: The seats attach to the frame with tamper-resistant screws (T40 bit)

Applications

Structured Seating suits waiting areas and common rooms where seating has to stay in rows, stand up to heavy use, and be easy to clean.

Typical applications include:

  • Lobbies and waiting rooms in correctional facilities and police stations
  • Dayrooms and common areas in jails and detention centers
  • Behavioral healthcare facilities and other public waiting areas
