The Cortech Summit Veldura Lounge Chair is a molded lounge chair with a calm, residential look for patient rooms, dayrooms, lounges, and group spaces in behavioral healthcare and other secure settings. The Veldura soft-touch surface adds comfort and warmth without giving up durability.

The chair is rotationally molded in one piece from high impact “no break” polyethylene, with no steel or wood components. It can be weighted at the factory, or the 321S can be ballasted with sandbags on site.

  • Material: One-piece rotationally molded high impact “no break” polyethylene; no steel or wood components
  • Finish: Veldura soft-touch exterior that contrasts with the molded base
  • Dimensions: 29”W×59”D×35”H
  • Weight: 50 lbs
  • Weighting: Factory sand ballasting optional; the 321S has a ballast door for adding sandbags on site that closes with tamper-resistant screws. Do not use loose sand
  • Outdoor Use: Rated for outdoor use and UV stabilized
  • Antimicrobial Testing: Tested to ISO 22196, showing a greater than 99% reduction in bacteria
  • Flammability Standards: Passes UL 94 HB
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD Gold
  • Easy Cleaning: Sound dampening, and easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 321 – Veldura: Without ballast door
  • 321S – Veldura with Ballast Door: Ballast door for adding sandbags on site

Installation and Options

  • Weighting: Can be factory weighted, or add sandbags on site through the ballast door on the 321S; do not use loose sand. Hardware bit size is T27

Applications

The Summit Veldura Lounge Chair suits rooms that need comfortable, durable seating that is hard to break or tamper with.

Typical applications include:

  • Patient rooms, dayrooms, and lounges in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Group rooms and visitor areas in residential treatment centers
  • Other secure settings that want a calm, residential look
