The Norix Multi-Purpose Table with Square Top is a lighter-weight laminate table on a welded steel X-base, for classrooms, dayrooms, and program rooms in correctional and behavioral healthcare facilities.

It comes in 36” and 42” square sizes, with a plain laminate top or a game top.

The base is 1¾” 12-gauge steel tubing, fully welded and sealed on the exposed ends, so there are no openings to hide contraband. It is powder coated for scratch and corrosion resistance, and tamper-resistant glides allow leveling. The top fastens to the base with tamper-resistant security screws for a solid metal-to-metal connection.

  • Base: Steel X-base of 1¾” 12-gauge tubing, fully welded and sealed, with tamper-resistant leveling glides
  • Top: Decorative high-pressure laminate over a 45 lb density M3 particle board core with embedded T-nuts and a backer sheet, edged with glued and pinned black vinyl T-molding, 1¼” thick
  • Top Sizes: 36” or 42” square
  • Height: 29”
  • Weight: 63 lbs (36”) or 79 lbs (42”)
  • Plain Top Finishes: Titanium Evolve, Wild Cherry, Calcutta Marble, or Black Alicante with a black edge, or Kensington Maple, Asian Sun, Montana Walnut, or Palisades Oak with a bark edge
  • Game Top: Optional Titanium Evolve laminate with a printed game board, black edge
  • Base Colors: Black or metallic silver
  • Static Load Testing: Tested to 500 lbs
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD and GREENGUARD Gold
  • Warranty: 10-year limited replacement warranty
  • Intended Use: Indoor use only
  • Country of Manufacture: USA

Available Models and Options

  • MP3636 – 36” Square: Plain top or game top, 63 lbs
  • MP4242 – 42” Square: Plain top or game top, 79 lbs

Applications

The Norix Multi-Purpose Square Table suits classrooms, dayrooms, and program rooms that need a lighter table that is still tamper resistant.

Typical applications include:

  • Dayrooms and dining halls in correctional and detention facilities
  • Behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Visitation areas, classrooms, and other common areas

About Norix Dayroom and Dining Tables

Norix tables are made for dayrooms, dining halls, classrooms, and visitation areas in correctional and behavioral healthcare facilities, where furniture is used hard every day. The range covers molded-base tables such as the Jupiter, Evvolve, and Molded X-Base; Laminate X-Base tables; and steel-base tables such as the Leg Style, Multi-Purpose, Max-Master, and Oasis.

Common characteristics of Norix tables include:

  • Durable Tops: High-pressure laminate over a dense particle board core, with molded or T-mold edges depending on the model
  • Secure Connections: Tops attach to the base with tamper-resistant hardware for a solid metal-to-metal connection
  • Placement Options: Depending on the model, tables can be ballasted, bolted to the floor, or set on tamper-resistant glides
  • Easy Cleaning: Smooth surfaces that stand up to routine cleaning in high-traffic areas
  • Made in the USA: Built by Norix in the United States
