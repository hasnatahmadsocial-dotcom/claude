The Norix Respite Chaise Lounge is a one-piece, cushioned lounge chair designed with trauma-informed principles for behavioral healthcare and crisis care rooms. Its gentle recline gives patients a calm, secure place to rest, and it stays comfortable during extended use. An integrated pillow with slightly curved sides supports the head and neck and gives patients a sense of privacy, while still letting staff monitor them.

The flexible PVC skin is filled with foam and fastened with tamper-resistant fasteners to a molded polyethylene base, with no internal openings, creases, or nooks. A concave surface at the base lets caregivers position their feet close to the chair, so they can help with patient transfers and reach the patient during medical treatment.

  • Construction: One-piece, rotationally molded 0.25” flexible polyvinyl chloride skin with a softer durometer for a comfortable sit
  • Fill and Base: Flexible 2-part water-based polyurethane foam, attached with tamper-resistant fasteners to a linear low-density polyethylene (LLDPE) base
  • Integrated Pillow: Curved sides support the head and neck and give a sense of privacy
  • Concave Base: Lets caregivers position their feet for safer patient transfers and easy access during treatment
  • Ligature Resistant: No internal openings
  • Rounded Edges: Smooth, rounded edges for a safe design
  • Chemical Resistance: Resists blood, vinegar, urine, feces, salt solution, and chlorine solution
  • Overall Size: 26”W×64.3”D×34.5”H
  • Seat: 15.6” seat height and 17.7” seat width, with a 21” arm height
  • Weight: 130 lbs
  • Colors: Twilight, Sage, Clay, Sienna, Dove, or Raven
  • Static Load Testing: Tested to 750 lbs
  • Performance Standards: Dynamic drop tested to twice the ANSI/BIFMA X5.4 impact force (250 lbs from 18”); tested to 184 lbs puncture resistance (ASTM D4833-07); meets or exceeds ANSI/BIFMA X5.4
  • Flammability Standards: California Technical Bulletins 133 and 117
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD and GREENGUARD Gold
  • Cleaning: Purple Power cleaner for routine cleaning; World’s Best Sensitive Surface Graffiti Remover for tougher stains; a thin layer of Black Magic Protectant every two months
  • Care Note: Apparel dyes, such as denim, may transfer onto lighter colors, and ballpoint ink and permanent markers can stain
  • Warranty: 10-year limited replacement warranty
  • Intended Use: Indoor use only
  • Country of Manufacture: USA

Applications

The Norix Respite Chaise Lounge suits rooms where patients need a calm, safe place to rest, and where staff need to monitor and reach them easily.

Typical applications include:

  • Crisis care and crisis stabilization units
  • Comfort rooms and calming rooms in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Dayrooms, lounges, and waiting areas
