The Norix Progress Stackable Chair is a passive ergonomic chair with a live hinge molded into the backrest, so the back flexes for comfort in both upright and relaxed sitting positions. The one-piece seat shell sits on black powder-coated aluminum legs, and the chairs nest when stacked for compact storage.

The simple design has no nuts, bolts, welds, or rivets to tamper with. Norix recommends the Progress Chair for staff use and monitored programming rooms, such as classrooms, training rooms, offices, and lobbies.

  • Seat Shell: One-piece, injection-molded high-impact grade copolymer polypropylene, with an anti-static inhibitor added when molded
  • Flexible Backrest: A live hinge molded into the backrest gives the flexibility needed for long-term comfort
  • Legs: Black powder-coated aluminum with friction fit foot caps
  • Tamper Resistant: No nuts, bolts, welds, or rivets
  • Stackable: The leg shape lets the chairs nest when stacked
  • Seat Colors: Twilight, Cove, Sand, or Dove
  • Dimensions: 19.7”W×23.2”D×32”H, with a 17.3” seat height
  • Weight: 10 lbs
  • Static Load Testing: Tested to 500 lbs
  • Performance Standards: ANSI/BIFMA X5.1 2002 General Purpose Office Chairs; ISO 14001:2015
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD and GREENGUARD Gold
  • Flammability: Not California Technical Bulletin 133 compliant
  • Cleaning: Simple Green or a similar cleaner for routine cleaning, and Clorox Clean-Up Cleaner + Bleach for tougher stains; soap and water for the legs
  • Warranty: 10-year limited replacement warranty
  • Intended Use: Indoor use only

Available Models and Options

  • C920 – Progress Chair: Armless stackable chair in Twilight, Cove, Sand, or Dove, 10 lbs
  • Upholstered Option: The Progress Chair is also available with upholstery, in 8 Norix fabric grades

Installation and Options

  • No assembly is required

Applications

The Norix Progress Chair suits supervised areas where staff and visitors need comfortable seating that stacks away when the room is cleared. It is not suitable for confined areas.

Typical applications include:

  • Staff offices, break rooms, and training rooms
  • Classrooms and monitored programming rooms in behavioral healthcare and correctional facilities
  • Lobbies and waiting areas
  • Police and fire stations
