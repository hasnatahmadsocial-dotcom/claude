The Cortech Tiger Plate Security Wall Plate Covers cover light switches, outlets, and GFI receptacles in correctional and behavioral healthcare facilities, where standard wall plates are easily broken or pried off. They come in eight single and double gang types.

Each plate is injection molded in one piece from a high strength polycarbonate and nylon blend, so it will not rust or break and never needs painting. The plates are oversized to mount directly to any wall type and to cover the junction box opening and uneven wall surfaces.

  • Material: One-piece injection molded high strength polycarbonate and nylon blend with a textured finish; 3/16” wall thickness
  • Sizes: Single gang 3¾”W×5¾”H; double gang 5¾”W×5¾”H (oversized)
  • Durability: High impact resistance; virtually unbreakable and 100% rust proof
  • Protective Lip: Side impact lip protects the edges of the plate
  • Corners: Smooth radius corners
  • Fastening: Tamper-resistant fastening system; four zinc plated Torx security screws with center pin are included
  • Color: Smudge resistant white (custom colors available on request)
  • Wiring Devices: Not included
  • Care: Stain resistant; clean with a damp cloth
  • Warranty: 10-year factory warranty
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • Single Gang: TPSS - Single Switch, TPDR - Duplex Receptacle, TPBB - Blank, TPGF - Ground Fault Interrupter Receptacle
  • Double Gang: TPDBB - Double Blank, TPDD - Double Duplex Receptacle, TPDGF - Double Ground Fault Interrupter Receptacles, TPDS - Duplex Switch

Installation and Options

  • Mounting: Attach the wiring device to the plate with the tamperproof screws and Nyloc nuts provided, then fasten the plate to the wall with the security screws; use Tapcon concrete screws on concrete walls; see Cortech instructions
  • Hardware Bit Size: T27 for the corner screws; T15 for the machine screws

Applications

Tiger Plates suit any area where residents could break or tamper with standard wall plates.

Typical applications include:

  • Cells, dayrooms, and common areas in correctional facilities and jails
  • Patient rooms, bathrooms, and common areas in behavioral healthcare facilities
  • Schools, shelters, and other high-abuse settings
