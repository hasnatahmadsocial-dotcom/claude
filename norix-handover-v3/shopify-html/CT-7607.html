The Cortech Endurance Floor Mounted Desk, also called the Freestanding Desk or Floor Mount Desk, is a one-piece molded desk that can stand on its own or be bolted to the floor. It is made for cells, patient rooms, and other rooms in intense use environments.

The desk is rotationally molded from high impact “no break” polyethylene, with no steel or wood components. It comes all plastic (7607) or with a smooth laminate top (7608) in a choice of standard Formica and Wilsonart finishes for a more normative look.

  • Material: One-piece rotationally molded high impact “no break” polyethylene; no steel or wood components
  • Dimensions: 36”W×24”D×29”H
  • Weight: 47 lbs
  • Top: All plastic (7607) or optional smooth laminate top (7608)
  • Laminate Top: Smooth laminate top in a choice of standard Formica and Wilsonart finishes
  • Mounting: Freestanding, or bolts to the floor through concealed pilot holes
  • Antimicrobial Testing: Tested to ISO 22196, showing a greater than 99% reduction in bacteria
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133 and UL 94 HB
  • Easy Cleaning: Sound dampening, and easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Base Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty on the all-plastic model; 5-year warranty on the laminate top
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 7607 – All Plastic: 36”W×24”D×29”H, 47 lbs
  • 7608 – Laminate Top: 36”W×24”D×29”H, smooth laminate top, 47 lbs

Installation and Options

  • Mounting: Use freestanding, or bolt to the floor through concealed pilot holes; see Cortech instructions. Hardware not included

Applications

The Floor Mounted Desk suits rooms that need a durable desk, either freestanding or fixed to the floor.

Typical applications include:

  • Cells and housing units in correctional facilities and jails
  • Patient rooms in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Residential treatment centers, classrooms, and study areas

About Cortech Endurance Series

The Cortech Endurance Series is a line of heavy-duty molded furniture for behavioral healthcare facilities, correctional institutions, and other intense use environments. Endurance products are made for durability, easy cleaning, and safety-conscious design.

The Endurance Series includes:

  • Beds and Bunks: Endurance beds, wall-mounted bunks, and EZ Bunk stackable temporary sleep surfaces
  • Seating: Benches, cubes, and the Endurance ‘In Sync’ Modular Series chairs
  • Tables and Desks: Molded and laminate-top tables, freestanding desks, and wall-mounted desks, including ADA models
  • Storage: Lockers, nightstands, open chests, and shelving
