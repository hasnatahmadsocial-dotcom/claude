The Cortech SafeBin Lockable Trash Receptacle is a molded trash receptacle for behavioral healthcare and correctional facilities. Its tamper-resistant waste chute keeps the liner and discarded items out of reach, and an integrated lock with a master key keeps the bin closed.

The SafeBin is rotationally molded without metal hinges, bolts to the floor or wall, and holds a 35-gallon inner container that can be used with or without trash liners. It is rated for outdoor use.

  • Construction: Rotationally molded, with no metal hinges
  • Capacity: 35-gallon inner container, for use with or without trash liners
  • Waste Chute: Tamper-resistant chute prevents access to the liner and discarded items
  • Lock: Integrated lock with master key
  • Mounting: Bolts to the floor or wall
  • Cleaning: Slightly sloped bottom interior for easy cleaning and drainage
  • Weight: 115 lbs
  • Outdoor Use: Rated for outdoor use and UV stabilized
  • Colors: Black top with a body in Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: 10-year warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 35 – SafeBin: Body color of your choice with a black top

Installation and Options

  • Floor or Wall Mounting: Bolt to the floor or wall through four pilot holes; concrete anchors, hex bolts, and washers are included; see Cortech instructions

Applications

The SafeBin suits secure areas where staff need to keep trash and liners out of residents’ reach.

Typical applications include:

  • Housing units, dayrooms, common areas and outdoor spaces in correctional facilities and jails
  • Patient areas in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Kitchens and other secure areas that need a locked trash receptacle
