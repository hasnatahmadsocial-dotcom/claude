The Norix Jupiter Cylinder Base Table with Round Top pairs a molded polyethylene cylinder base with a high-pressure laminate top, for dayrooms, dining areas, and classrooms in correctional and behavioral healthcare facilities. It comes in 36”, 42”, and 48” round sizes, with a plain laminate top or a game top.

The base is rotationally molded from high-impact polyethylene with UV stabilizers and has a lightly textured surface that is easy to clean with bleach solutions and hospital-grade disinfectants. The cylinder base is made to be weighted on site with up to 200 lbs of dry sand or gravel, or Norix can ballast it at the factory. Ballast or a bolt-down kit is required to validate the warranty.

  • Base: Rotationally molded high-impact polyethylene cylinder with UV stabilizers, color Raven
  • Top: High-pressure laminate over a 45 lb density M3 particle board core, with a 1½” thick radiused molded edge that is pressure fused to the top
  • Top Attachment: Embedded T-nuts give a steel-to-steel connection between the top and base
  • Top Sizes: 36”, 42”, or 48” round
  • Height: 29.3”
  • Weight: 68 to 103 lbs, depending on size (without ballast)
  • Plain Top Finishes: Titanium Evolve, Wild Cherry, Kensington Maple, Asian Sun, Montana Walnut, Palisades Oak, Calcutta Marble, or Black Alicante laminate with a matching or black molded edge
  • Game Top: Optional Titanium Evolve laminate with a printed game board, black molded edge
  • Static Load Testing: Tested to 1,000 lbs
  • Flammability Standards: California Technical Bulletin 133, UL 1056, and ASTM E1537
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD and GREENGUARD Gold
  • Warranty: 10-year limited replacement warranty
  • Intended Use: Indoor use only
  • Country of Manufacture: USA

Available Models and Options

  • CY3600 – 36” Round: Plain top or game top, 68 lbs
  • CY4200 – 42” Round: Plain top or game top, 82 lbs
  • CY4800 – 48” Round: Plain top or game top, 103 lbs

Installation and Options

  • Ballasting: Fill the base on site with up to 200 lbs of dry sand or gravel (not supplied), or order factory ballasting. Ballast or a bolt-down kit is required to validate the warranty.
  • Assembly: Some assembly required; see Norix instructions

Applications

The Norix Jupiter Round Table suits dayrooms and dining areas that need a heavy, stable table that can still be moved when the room layout changes.

Typical applications include:

  • Dayrooms and dining halls in correctional and detention facilities
  • Behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Visitation areas, classrooms, and other common areas

About Norix Dayroom and Dining Tables

Norix tables are made for dayrooms, dining halls, classrooms, and visitation areas in correctional and behavioral healthcare facilities, where furniture is used hard every day. The range covers molded-base tables such as the Jupiter, Evvolve, and Molded X-Base; Laminate X-Base tables; and steel-base tables such as the Leg Style, Multi-Purpose, Max-Master, and Oasis.

Common characteristics of Norix tables include:

  • Durable Tops: High-pressure laminate over a dense particle board core, with molded or T-mold edges depending on the model
  • Secure Connections: Tops attach to the base with tamper-resistant hardware for a solid metal-to-metal connection
  • Placement Options: Depending on the model, tables can be ballasted, bolted to the floor, or set on tamper-resistant glides
  • Easy Cleaning: Smooth surfaces that stand up to routine cleaning in high-traffic areas
  • Made in the USA: Built by Norix in the United States
