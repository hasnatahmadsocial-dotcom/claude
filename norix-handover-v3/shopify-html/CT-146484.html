The Cortech Radial Rocker Chair is a molded rocking chair for behavioral healthcare and correctional facilities. Its gentle, controlled rocking motion helps users relax, and its low center of gravity keeps the chair stable and the motion smooth.

The rocker is rotationally molded in one piece from high impact “no break” polyethylene, with no steel or wood components, and can be ballasted with sand for safety (model 146484S).

  • Material: One-piece rotationally molded high impact “no break” polyethylene; no steel or wood components
  • Dimensions: 30”W×45”D×40”H
  • Weight: 60 lbs unfilled
  • Rocking Radius: 56°
  • Stability: Lower center of gravity improves stability and creates a smoother sway
  • Ballast Door: The 146484S has a ballast door for adding sandbags on site; it closes with tamper-resistant screws. Do not use loose sand
  • Outdoor Use: Rated for outdoor use and UV stabilized
  • Antimicrobial Testing: Tested to ISO 22196, showing a greater than 99% reduction in bacteria
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133 and UL 94 HB
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD Gold
  • Easy Cleaning: Sound dampening, and easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 146484 – Standard: Without access door
  • 146484S – With Access Door: Ballast door for adding sandbags on site

Installation and Options

  • Weighting: Add sandbags through the ballast door on the 146484S; do not use loose sand. Hardware bit size is T27

Applications

The Radial Rocker suits calming spaces where a safe rocking chair helps users relax.

Typical applications include:

  • Lounges and dayrooms in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Dayrooms in correctional facilities and juvenile detention centers
  • Residential treatment centers and other therapeutic settings
