The Cortech Razorback Stackable Chair is a virtually unbreakable stack chair for dayrooms, dining areas, classrooms, and group rooms in behavioral healthcare and correctional facilities. Its low back and Muscle-Back legs make it harder to rock and tip over, and the chairs stack for storage.

The chair is injection molded in one piece from high impact “no break” polypropylene, with no steel or wood components. It weighs just over 8 lbs, has a 1,500 lb static load, and its legs have a bolt-down option. The Razorback is rated for indoor or outdoor use.

  • Material: One-piece injection molded high impact “no break” polypropylene; no steel or wood components
  • Dimensions: 21”W×21”D×30”H
  • Weight: 8.25 lbs
  • Static Load: 1,500 lbs (ANSI/BIFMA X5.1-2011)
  • Stacking: Stack 8 to 10 chairs on a dolly, or as high as you can comfortably reach without one; chair dolly sold separately
  • Legs: Muscle-Back legs reduce rocking and tipping and include a bolt-down option
  • Testing: Passes ASTM F1561-03
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133 and UL 94 HB
  • Outdoor Use: Suitable for indoor or outdoor use; resists UV exposure and moisture
  • Easy Cleaning: Easy to clean with a damp cloth; resistant to stains
  • Colors: Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Buff, Fog Gray, Green, Plum, Slate Blue, or Teal (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

Installation and Options

  • Floor Mounting: Optional; the Muscle-Back legs have a pilot hole for field drilling to bolt the chair down. Hardware not included

Applications

The Razorback suits rooms that need light, stackable chairs that are hard to break, rock, or tip.

Typical applications include:

  • Dayrooms, dining areas, and visitation rooms in correctional facilities and jails
  • Group rooms and activity rooms in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Schools, shelters, and other intense use settings
