The Cortech Barracuda Box Inmate Property Storage Box gives jails and prisons a tough, lightweight box for inmate property and records. Each box is molded in one piece from high impact “no break” polypropylene, with rounded corners and edges and vertical structural columns that help it stand up to impact.

The stack-and-nest design lets you stack boxes, with or without a lid, without crushing what is inside. Turn an empty box 180° and it nests inside another, which saves storage space and freight. There is a locking hasp on both sides of the box, and the matching Barracuda Box lid is sold separately.

  • Material: One-piece injection-molded high impact “no break” polypropylene
  • Construction: Rounded corners and edges and vertical structural columns for strength and impact resistance
  • Stacking: Stacks with or without a lid; empty boxes nest when turned 180°
  • Locking: Locking hasp on both sides of the box
  • Capacity: 1.5, 2.0, or 3.0 cu ft
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133
  • Color: Gray (custom colors available on request)
  • Lid: Sold separately as the Cortech Barracuda Box Lid
  • Easy Cleaning: Easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Warranty: 10-year factory warranty
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Applications

The Barracuda Box is made for allowable in-cell storage and property rooms. With three sizes, facilities can choose the size that fits what each person is allowed to keep.

Typical applications include:

  • Prisons, jails, and detention centers
  • Allowable in-cell property storage
  • Inmate records and personal property storage

About Cortech Property Storage Boxes

Cortech property storage boxes give jails, prisons, and other secure facilities a durable way to store inmate property and records. Both box lines are molded from high impact “no break” polypropylene in the same three sizes.
