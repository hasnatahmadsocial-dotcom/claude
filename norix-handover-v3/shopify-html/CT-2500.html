The Cortech Rock 2.0 Insulated Food Tray is a seamless, one-piece meal tray for prisons, jails, and other correctional facilities. It has the same footprint as the original Rock but deeper food compartments, and it uses the same Rock lids.

The tray is molded from high impact “no break” polypropylene made with FDA-approved material. It has five insulated food compartments and two condiment compartments for bulk condiments, does not absorb water, and stacks with or without lids. The Rock 2.0 fits the Chuckwagon, Chuckwagon Jr., Chuckwagon Max, and Tray Master delivery carts.

  • Material: One-piece, seamless high impact “no break” polypropylene; does not absorb water
  • Compartments: Five insulated food compartments and two condiment compartments for bulk condiments
  • Dimensions: 14¼”L×9½”W×2¼”H; food compartments are 1¾” deep
  • Weight: 2 lbs per tray
  • Corners: Radius corners and a softly beveled bottom make it easier to eat from and to clean
  • Stacking: Stacks with or without lids; uses the same lids as the Rock
  • Cart Capacity: 120 Rock 2.0 Trays (84 with lids) in the Chuckwagon, 68 (48 with lids) in the Chuckwagon Jr., or 114 in the Tray Master
  • Food Safety: Made with FDA-approved material
  • Care: Do not use bleach on the trays
  • Colors: Brown, Dark Gray, Terracotta, or Orange (custom colors available on request)
  • Packaging: Sold in cases of 10. Price is per case
  • Warranty: 5-year factory warranty
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • Rock 2.0 Tray: 2500C - Brown, 2500DG - Dark Gray, 2500TC - Terracotta, 2500O - Orange
  • Lids: Rock lids (2000CL, 2000DGL, 2000TCL, and 2000OL) sold separately

Applications

The Rock 2.0 suits facilities that need deeper compartments but want to keep the Rock footprint and lids.

Typical applications include:

  • Inmate meal service in prisons, jails, and detention centers
  • Central kitchens that deliver meals to housing units
  • Juvenile detention and other secure facilities
