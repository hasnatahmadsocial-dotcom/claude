The Norix Leg Style Table with Square Top is a four-leg laminate table built for continuous daily use in dayrooms, dining areas, and classrooms in correctional and behavioral healthcare facilities. It comes in 36” and 42” square sizes with a plain laminate top or a game top, and 4” legs or 2½” legs for a more residential look.

Each leg is steel tubing welded to a steel mounting plate and fastened to the top with tamper-resistant screws and embedded T-nuts, for a steel-to-steel connection. The radiused molded edge is permanently fused to the top, which makes it comfortable and well suited to food service. Legs come with tamper-resistant floor glides, and bolt-down is available.

  • Legs: 4” or 2½” diameter, 14-gauge steel tubing welded to a 6”×6”×¼” steel mounting plate, with tamper-resistant adjustable leveling glides
  • Top: High-pressure laminate over a 45 lb density M3 particle board core, with a 1½” thick radiused molded edge that is pressure fused to the top
  • Top Sizes: 36” or 42” square
  • Height: 29”
  • Weight: 107 to 115 lbs (LS3636) or 121 to 137 lbs (LS4242), depending on leg size
  • Plain Top Finishes: Titanium Evolve, Wild Cherry, Kensington Maple, Asian Sun, Montana Walnut, Palisades Oak, Calcutta Marble, or Black Alicante laminate with a matching or black molded edge
  • Game Top: Optional Titanium Evolve laminate with a printed game board, black molded edge
  • Leg Colors: Black or metallic silver
  • Static Load Testing: Tested to 1,000 lbs
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD and GREENGUARD Gold
  • Warranty: 10-year limited replacement warranty
  • Intended Use: Indoor use only
  • Country of Manufacture: USA

Available Models and Options

  • LS3636 – 36” Square: Plain top or game top, up to 115 lbs
  • LS4242 – 42” Square: Plain top or game top, up to 137 lbs

Installation and Options

  • Glides or Bolt-Down: Tamper-resistant leveling glides are standard. Bolt-down is optional, with hardware included.
  • Assembly: See Norix instructions

Applications

The Norix Leg Style Square Table suits dayrooms and dining areas that need a durable table for small groups.

About Norix Dayroom and Dining Tables

Norix tables are made for dayrooms, dining halls, classrooms, and visitation areas in correctional and behavioral healthcare facilities, where furniture is used hard every day. The range covers molded-base tables such as the Jupiter, Evvolve, and Molded X-Base; Laminate X-Base tables; and steel-base tables such as the Leg Style, Multi-Purpose, Max-Master, and Oasis.

Common characteristics of Norix tables include:

  • Durable Tops: High-pressure laminate over a dense particle board core, with molded or T-mold edges depending on the model
  • Secure Connections: Tops attach to the base with tamper-resistant hardware for a solid metal-to-metal connection
  • Placement Options: Depending on the model, tables can be ballasted, bolted to the floor, or set on tamper-resistant glides
  • Easy Cleaning: Smooth surfaces that stand up to routine cleaning in high-traffic areas
  • Made in the USA: Built by Norix in the United States
