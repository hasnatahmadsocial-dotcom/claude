The Cortech Solstice Bed is a one-piece molded bed with a home-like look for patient rooms in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals. Raised, molded-in head and footboards keep the mattress from sliding, and the bed fits a 36”×75” mattress.

The bed is rotationally molded as a single unit from high impact “no break” polyethylene, with no seams, steel, or wood. It can be bolted to the floor, or the 100S3 can be weighted with sandbags.

  • Material: One-piece rotationally molded high impact “no break” polyethylene; no steel or wood components
  • Dimensions: 80”L×36”W×21”H
  • Weight: 120 lbs
  • Mattress: Fits a 36”×75” mattress (sold separately)
  • Head and Footboards: Molded-in, raised head and footboards keep the mattress from sliding
  • Storage: No shelves or storage
  • Ballast Doors: The 100S3 has underside ballast doors for adding sandbags; do not use loose sand
  • Floor Mounting: Can be bolted to the floor through concealed pilot holes
  • Antimicrobial Testing: Tested to ISO 22196, showing a greater than 99% reduction in bacteria
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD Gold
  • Easy Cleaning: Easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 100 – Standard: Without ballast doors
  • 100S3 – With Ballast Doors: Underside ballast doors for adding sandbags

Installation and Options

  • Floor Mounting: Bolt to the floor through concealed pilot holes. Hardware not included
  • Ballast Doors: Add sandbags through the underside ballast doors on the 100S3; do not use loose sand. Hardware bit size is T27

Applications

The Solstice Bed suits patient rooms that need a safe, strong bed with a calm, residential look.

Typical applications include:

  • Patient rooms in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Residential treatment centers and other secure facilities
  • Cells and housing units in correctional facilities
