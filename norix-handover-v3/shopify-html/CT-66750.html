The Cortech Endurance X-Base Molded Table with Square Top is a fully molded 42” square table for dining rooms, dayrooms, and activity rooms in correctional and behavioral healthcare facilities. The top and the x-shaped base are rotationally molded from high impact “no break” polyethylene, and the rounded edge of the top adds safety and comfort.

The legs attach to the top with tamper-resistant screws. The table can be bolted to the floor through concealed pilot holes or the legs can be filled with sand to weigh it down. It comes with a plain top or a game top, and it is rated for outdoor use.

  • Material: Top and base rotationally molded from high impact “no break” polyethylene with an antimicrobial compound
  • Base: Molded x-shaped base; the legs attach to the top with tamper-resistant screws
  • Top Size: 42”×42” square, with a rounded edge
  • Dimensions: 42”W×42”D×29”H
  • Weight: 59 lbs (top 27 lbs, base 32 lbs)
  • Game Top: Optional game top with a checkerboard pattern
  • Outdoor Use: Rated for outdoor use and UV stabilized
  • Antimicrobial Testing: Tested to ISO 22196, showing a greater than 99% reduction in bacteria
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133 and UL 94 HB
  • Easy Cleaning: Sound dampening, and easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 66750 – 42” Square, Plain Top: 59 lbs
  • 66750GT – 42” Square, Game Top: 59 lbs

Installation and Options

  • Floor Mounting: Bolt to the floor through concealed pilot holes with the Sabre Mount System
  • Ballast: The legs can be filled with sand to weigh the table down
  • Assembly: The two leg pieces slide together and attach to the top with tamper-resistant screws (T40 bit); see Cortech instructions

Applications

The Endurance X-Base Molded Table suits dining rooms, dayrooms, and activity rooms where a table has to stand up to hard daily use and frequent cleaning.

Typical applications include:

  • Dining rooms and dayrooms in correctional facilities and jails
  • Behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Residential treatment centers and activity rooms
