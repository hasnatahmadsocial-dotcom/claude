The Cortech Endurance Slim ADA Wall Mount Desk is a space-saving, ADA-compliant desk for compact cells, patient rooms, and other secure settings. It is designed to fit in a corner or on a standard wall. The desk is rotationally molded in one piece from high impact “no break” polyethylene, with no steel or wood components.

  • Material: One-piece rotationally molded high impact “no break” polyethylene; no steel or wood components
  • ADA Compliance: Space saving and ADA compliant
  • Dimensions: 30”W×19½”D×7½”H
  • Weight: 18 lbs
  • Mounting: Designed for corner or standard wall mounting; bolts to the wall through pilot holes
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133
  • Easy Cleaning: Easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 8700 – Slim ADA Desk: 30”W×19½”D×7½”H, 18 lbs

Installation and Options

  • Wall Mounting: Drill through the six pilot holes, mark the wall, and bolt the desk to the wall with six 3/8”-16×3¾” security Torx anchor bolts and flat washers in holes at least 2” deep; see Cortech instructions. Hardware not included
  • Pilot Holes: Cortech advises not to pre-drill pilot holes until the desk is on site and ready to install

Applications

The Slim ADA Desk suits small, accessible rooms where a desk must be fixed to the wall without taking up floor space.

Typical applications include:

  • Accessible cells and housing units in correctional facilities and jails
  • Accessible patient rooms in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Residential treatment centers and other rooms that need an ADA-compliant desk
