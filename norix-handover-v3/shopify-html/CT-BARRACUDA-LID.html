The Cortech Barracuda Box Inmate Property Storage Box Lid closes off a Cortech Barracuda Box so it can be locked and stacked. The lid slides open from the short side of the box, so staff can reach more of the contents without taking the lid off, and a full perimeter stacking groove keeps stacked boxes in place.

Like the boxes, the lids are molded in one piece from high impact “no break” polypropylene. There are two sizes: a universal lid for the 1.5 and 2.0 cu ft boxes, and a lid for the 3.0 cu ft box. The box is sold separately.

  • Material: One-piece injection-molded high impact “no break” polypropylene
  • Handles: Molded-in handles for opening and closing
  • Access: Slides open on the short side of the box
  • Security: Full engagement edges on three sides help prevent prying when the box is locked
  • Stacking: Full perimeter stacking groove for stacking Barracuda Boxes
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133
  • Color: Gray (custom colors available on request)
  • Box: Sold separately as the Cortech Barracuda Box
  • Warranty: 10-year factory warranty
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 9689P – Universal Lid: Fits the 3825P and 7268P boxes (1.5 and 2.0 cu ft), 23½”W×17½”D×1½”H
  • 8289P – Extra Large Lid: Fits the 8568P box (3.0 cu ft), 29”W×18½”D×1½”H

No assembly is required.

Applications

Adding a lid lets facilities lock Barracuda Boxes and stack them neatly, whether the boxes are kept in cells or in a property room.

Typical applications include:

  • Prisons, jails, and detention centers
  • Allowable in-cell property storage
  • Inmate records and personal property storage

About Cortech Property Storage Boxes

Cortech property storage boxes give jails, prisons, and other secure facilities a durable way to store inmate property and records. Both box lines are molded from high impact “no break” polypropylene in the same three sizes.

Cortech property storage includes:

  • Barracuda Box: Stack-and-nest property boxes in 1.5, 2.0, and 3.0 cu ft sizes, with a locking hasp on both sides
  • Master Box: Lockable property boxes in the same three sizes, with a built-in combination dial lock and master override key
  • Lids: Matching Barracuda Box and Master Box lids in two sizes, sold separately
