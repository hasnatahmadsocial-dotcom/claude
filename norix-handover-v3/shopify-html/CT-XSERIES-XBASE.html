The Cortech Endurance X-Series Laminate Table with X-Base pairs a laminate top with the rotationally molded Endurance X-base, for dayrooms and dining areas in correctional and behavioral healthcare facilities. The x-shaped base carries heavy loads without tipping and helps keep chairs and people apart.

The seamless laminate top resists impact and has a rounded bull nose Dura-Edge for safety and comfort. The legs fasten to the top with tamper-resistant screws and a metal-to-metal connection, and the table can be bolted to the floor through concealed pilot holes or weighed down by filling the legs with sand. Tops come in 36” and 42” square and 42” and 48” round, with a plain top or a game top.

  • Top: Seamless laminate top with high impact resistance and a rounded bull nose Dura-Edge
  • Base: Rotationally molded high impact “no break” polyethylene X-base
  • Connection: The legs fasten to the top with tamper-resistant screws and a metal-to-metal connection
  • Top Sizes: 36”×36” or 42”×42” square, or 42” or 48” round
  • Game Top: Optional on the 36” and 42” square and the 48” round tables
  • Bull Nose Edge Colors: Dura-Edge in Atlantis, Black, Burgundy, Concrete, Dove Grey, Gray, Hunter Green, or Sienna
  • Base Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133
  • Easy Cleaning: Sound dampening, and easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty on the base; 5-year warranty on the laminate top
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

  • 42SDPX / 42SDGX – 42”×42” Square: Plain or game top, Dura-Edge

  • Vinyl Edge Models: 36SVPX, 36SVGX, 42SVPX, 42SVGX, 42RVPX, 48RVPX, and 48RVGX

Installation and Options

  • Floor Mounting: Bolt to the floor through concealed pilot holes with the Sabre Mount System
  • Ballast: The legs can be filled with sand to weigh the table down
  • Assembly: The two leg pieces slide together and attach to the top with tamper-resistant screws (T40 bit); see Cortech instructions

Applications

The X-Series Laminate Table suits dayrooms and dining areas that want the look of a laminate top on a heavy, stable molded base.

Typical applications include:

  • Dining rooms and dayrooms in correctional facilities and jails
  • Behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Residential treatment centers and activity rooms
