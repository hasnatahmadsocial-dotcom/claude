The Cortech Endurance Wall Mount Workstation Seat is a space-saving seat for compact cells, patient rooms, and other secure settings. It mounts directly to the wall and can be paired with the Endurance Workstation Desk or used on its own as a seat or shelf.

The seat is rotationally molded in one piece from high impact “no break” polyethylene, with no steel or wood components.

  • Material: One-piece rotationally molded high impact “no break” polyethylene; no steel or wood components
  • Use: Pairs with the Endurance Workstation Desk (8601) or other desks, or can be used as a shelf
  • Dimensions: 18”W×18”D×11”H
  • Weight: 15 lbs
  • Load Testing: 2,000 lb static load tested
  • Mounting: Bolts to the wall through concealed pilot holes
  • Outdoor Use: Rated for outdoor use and UV stabilized
  • Antimicrobial Testing: Tested to ISO 22196, showing a greater than 99% reduction in bacteria
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133 and UL 94 HB
  • Easy Cleaning: Sound dampening, and easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 8602 – Workstation Seat: 18”W×18”D×11”H, 15 lbs; desk sold separately

Installation and Options

  • Wall Mounting: Bolt to the wall through concealed pilot holes. Hardware not included

Applications

The Workstation Seat suits small rooms where seating must be fixed to the wall and floor space is limited.

Typical applications include:

  • Cells and housing units in correctional facilities and jails
  • Patient rooms in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Compact rooms and study areas where floor space is limited
