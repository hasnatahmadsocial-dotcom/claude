The Cortech Ventura Acrylic Mirror is a one-piece mirror made from 100% virgin acrylic, with the frame molded in. It has the look of a glass mirror without the same fragility, and it is highly resistant to breakage, which suits correctional and behavioral health facilities.

Each mirror comes with 10 tamper-resistant Torx button-head screws with center pin reject and has recessed mounting holes for secure attachment. It ships with an abrasion-resistant backing that protects the mirror surface.

  • Material: 100% virgin acrylic, one-piece design with integral frame
  • Reflection: High quality mirrored surface
  • Breakage: Highly resistant to breakage
  • Fasteners: 10 tamper-resistant Torx button-head screws with center pin reject, included (T25 bit)
  • Mounting: Recessed mounting holes
  • Protection: Ships with abrasion- and scratch-resistant backing material
  • Sizes: 11”×15”, 15”×20”, or 18”×24”
  • Cleaning: Clean with a microfiber cloth and non-abrasive soap and water; do not use glass cleaner
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • VM1115 – 11”×15”
  • VM1520 – 15”×20”
  • VM1824 – 18”×24”

Installation and Options

  • Mounting: Hold the mirror to the wall and mark the holes, drill ¼” holes 1” to 1⅛” deep for the anchors, and fit the protective O-ring between each screw and the mirror; tamper-resistant Torx screws included. See Cortech instructions
  • Tightening: Do not over-tighten the screws, which can damage the acrylic
  • Protective Film: Remove the front film after the mirror is installed

Applications

The Ventura Mirror suits rooms where abuse or harsh conditions would break or damage a glass mirror.

Typical applications include:

  • Correctional facilities, jails, and detention centers
  • Behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Schools, universities, and public safety facilities
