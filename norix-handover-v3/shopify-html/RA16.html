The Norix RockSmart Rocking Chair is a one-piece, rotationally molded rocker designed for therapeutic rocking in behavioral healthcare and correctional facilities. Its generous arc gives a relaxing, safe rocking action, and rear bumpers mark the limits of safe rocking. The ligature-resistant design has no internal openings, creases, or nooks where contraband can be hidden.

The chair is molded from high-impact polyethylene with UV stabilizers and Norix TruColor polymer for consistent, lasting color. It comes in 16” and 18” sizes, each in a standard or heavier weight model, with or without nylon runners to protect floors. The seamless surface can be cleaned with bleach solutions and hospital-grade disinfectants.

  • Construction: One-piece, rotationally molded high-impact polyethylene with ultraviolet light stabilizers to reduce fading
  • TruColor Polymer: Fully compounded color for consistent color and durability
  • Rocking Action: Generous arc for a relaxing and safe rock; rear nylon bumpers mark the limits of safe rocking
  • Runners: Securely affixed nylon runners protect floors (models without runners are also available)
  • Ligature Resistant: No internal openings
  • Chemical Resistance: Resists blood, vinegar, urine, feces, salt solution, and chlorine solution
  • 16” Rocker Size: 25.5”W×40.2”D×41.4”H, with a 17.5” seat height and 20.5” seat width
  • 18” Rocker Size: 27”W×45”D×44”H, with an 18.5” seat height and 21.8” seat width
  • Weight: 49 lbs (16” standard), 51 lbs (18” standard), or 71 lbs (heavier weight models)
  • Colors: Twilight, Cove, Sage, Clay, Sand, or Dove
  • Static Load Testing: Tested to 1,000 lbs
  • Performance Standards: Dynamic drop tested to twice the ANSI/BIFMA X5.4 impact force (250 lbs from 18”)
  • Flammability Standards: California Technical Bulletins 133 and 117; UL 1056; ASTM E1537; UL 94 HB; NFPA 261
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD and GREENGUARD Gold
  • Cleaning: Simple Green or a similar cleaner for routine cleaning; bleach and water solutions and hospital-grade disinfectants such as Virex, Wexcide, or Oxivir Five 16 can be used per the manufacturer’s instructions
  • Safety Note: The rear bumpers deter backward tipping, but rocking aggressively may cause the chair to tip over; do not stand on the chair
  • Warranty: 10-year limited replacement warranty
  • Intended Use: Indoor use only
  • Country of Manufacture: USA

Applications

The Norix RockSmart Rocking Chair suits rooms where residents benefit from calming, rhythmic movement, in spaces where a standard rocker would not be safe.

Typical applications include:

  • Dayrooms, activity rooms, and group rooms in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Comfort rooms and calming rooms
  • Waiting areas and visitation rooms
  • Dayrooms and group rooms in correctional and detention facilities
