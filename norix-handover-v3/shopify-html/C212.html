Norix Boulder Armless Beam Seats give lobbies and waiting areas sturdy in-line seating for two, three, or four people. The frame is bent and welded tubular steel, and each seat sits on its own welded steel plate, so the unit stays solid under heavy daily use.

Each one-piece seat and back is rotationally molded from high-impact polyethylene and fastened to the frame through molded-in threaded steel inserts, which makes the seats hard to tamper with. The smooth surface resists blood, urine, feces, vinegar, salt solution, and chlorine solution, and it can be cleaned with bleach solutions and hospital-grade disinfectants.

  • Seats: One-piece rotationally molded high-impact polyethylene seat and back with UV stabilizers

  • Overall Dimensions: 25”D×31.4”H; width depends on the number of seats

About Norix Boulder Beam Seating

Norix Boulder beam seating has been used in lobbies and waiting areas for years. Each unit pairs a powder-coated tubular steel frame with one-piece molded polyethylene seats, so it holds up to heavy public use and is easy to keep clean.

Common characteristics of Boulder beam seating include:

  • Welded Steel Frame: Full circumference welds at every joint, with no nuts, bolts, or rivets to loosen over time
  • Tamper-Resistant Seats: Each seat is attached to an 11-gauge steel seat support with nine security screws
  • Seamless Seats: One-piece seat and back that does not absorb fluids
  • Easy Cleaning: Seats can be cleaned with bleach solutions and hospital-grade disinfectants
  • TruColor™ Technology: Fully compounded color for consistent color and durability
