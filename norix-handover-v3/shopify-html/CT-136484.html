The Cortech Endurance Bench is a one-piece molded 48” bench for intake, assessment, and holding areas in correctional facilities, and for waiting rooms, lobbies, and common areas in behavioral healthcare and other secure settings.

The bench is rotationally molded from high impact “no break” polyethylene, with no steel or wood components and no seams or fasteners to tamper with. It bolts to the floor through concealed pilot holes and optional cuff rings can be added to one or both ends.

  • Material: One-piece rotationally molded high impact “no break” polyethylene; no steel or wood components
  • Dimensions: 48”W×21”D×18”H
  • Weight: 35 lbs
  • Mounting: Bolts to the floor through concealed pilot holes (hardware not included); the bench cannot be weighted or filled
  • Cuff Rings: Optional field-installed cuff rings for one or both ends, sold separately
  • Antimicrobial Testing: Tested to ISO 22196, showing a greater than 99% reduction in bacteria
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133 and UL 94 HB
  • Environmental Certification: GREENGUARD Gold
  • Easy Cleaning: Sound dampening, and easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 136484 – Bench: 48”W×21”D×18”H, 35 lbs

Installation and Options

  • Floor Mounting: Bolt to the floor through 12 concealed pilot holes with 3/8”-16×3¾” security Torx anchor bolts and flat washers; see Cortech instructions. Hardware not included
  • Cuff Rings: Optional cuff rings install on one or both ends

Applications

The Endurance Bench suits intake, holding, and waiting areas where seating must stay fixed in place and resist tampering.

Typical applications include:

  • Intake, assessment, and holding areas in correctional facilities and jails
  • Waiting rooms and lobbies in behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Common areas in residential treatment centers and other secure facilities
