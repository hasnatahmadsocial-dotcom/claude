The Cortech Endurance Cascade Base Table brings biophilic design to dining rooms, dayrooms, and activity rooms. Its molded base follows the flow of water, and it is paired here with a matching 42” round molded top in a plain or game top.

The top and base are rotationally molded from high impact “no break” polyethylene, and the base attaches to the top with tamper-resistant screws. The table can be bolted to the floor or weighted with sandbags. It is rated for outdoor use.

  • Material: Top and base rotationally molded from high impact “no break” polyethylene
  • Base: Molded Cascade base that attaches to the top with tamper-resistant screws
  • Top: 42” round molded top
  • Dimensions: 42”W×42”D×29”H
  • Weight: 38 lbs (top 22 lbs, base 16 lbs)
  • Ballast: Optional ballast door, hidden under the top, for adding weight with sandbags; secured with tamper-resistant screws
  • Game Top: Optional molded game top (670GTS)
  • Outdoor Use: Rated for outdoor use and UV stabilized
  • Antimicrobial Testing: Tested to ISO 22196, showing a greater than 99% reduction in bacteria
  • Flammability Standards: Flame retardant; passes California Technical Bulletin 133 and UL 94 HB
  • Easy Cleaning: Sound dampening, and easy to clean, sanitize, and maintain
  • Colors: Aqua, Black, Blue Gray, Brown, Burgundy, Canvas, Claylight, Gray, Green, Indigo, Midnight Blue, Moss, Overcast, Sand, Seagrass, Slate Blue, Stone Gray, or Tide (custom colors available on request)
  • Warranty: Lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Country of Manufacture: USA, made to ISO quality standards

Available Models and Options

  • 670S – Plain Top: 42” round molded top, 38 lbs
  • 670GTS – Game Top: 42” round molded game top, 38 lbs
  • Available with laminate top (not recommended for outdoor use)

Installation and Options

  • Assembly: The base attaches to the top with tamper-resistant screws and washers (T40 bit); see Cortech instructions
  • Floor Mounting: Bolt to the floor through concealed pilot holes with the Sabre Mount System; drill holes at least 2” deep and use concrete anchors; see Cortech instructions. Hardware not included
  • Ballast: Remove the ballast door panel screws (T27 bit), add sandbags, and replace the door; do not use loose sand

Applications

The Cascade Base Table suits dining and recreation areas where a table has to stand up to hard daily use while looking less institutional.

Typical applications include:

  • Dining rooms and dayrooms in correctional facilities and jails
  • Behavioral healthcare facilities and psychiatric hospitals
  • Residential treatment centers and activity rooms
