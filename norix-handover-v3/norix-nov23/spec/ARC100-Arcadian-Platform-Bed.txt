Arcadian
Platform Bed
                                                                      Fabrication & Material
                                                                      Rotationally molded high impact polyethylene is specially formulated for superior
                                                                      durability and impact resistance. The seamless, one-piece construction is ligature
                                                                      resistant and has no internal openings for added safety and security. An inverted,
                                                                      internal rib structure supports overall strength and allows for minimal surface
                                                                      movement. Raised outer edges keep fluids contained and a gently crowned top
                                                                      allows fluids to drain towards the corners. Chemically resistant to blood, vinegar,
                                                                      urine, feces, salt solution and chlorine solution. Made in the U.S.A.
