2. Research and Verify Product Information

Before editing a PDP, review the product information available from the manufacturer, including its website, catalogs, specifications, and other technical documents.

Identify and verify:

  • Correct product name and manufacturer model numbers
  • Product-specific features and benefits
  • Materials and construction
  • Dimensions, weights, colors, and finishes
  • Available configurations, accessories, and options
  • Installation and maintenance requirements, where applicable
  • Manufacturer-supported certifications, testing, and warranty information
  • Appropriate applications and intended environments

Accuracy is essential.

Do not invent specifications, certifications, safety claims, or product features.

Do not assume that specifications or certifications for one Norix product apply to another, even within the same furniture series.

When manufacturer information is missing, unclear, or inconsistent, flag it for review rather than guessing.

3. Use AI to Assist with Writing and Editing

You are encouraged to use ChatGPT or another AI tool to help prepare the product descriptions.

AI can be used to:

  • Rewrite manufacturer information into clear, professional product descriptions
  • Improve readability and organization
  • Incorporate relevant SEO keywords naturally
  • Eliminate unnecessary repetition
  • Draft product titles, subtitles, meta titles, and meta descriptions
  • Organize features and specifications
  • Maintain a consistent writing style

When using AI, provide it with the manufacturer information, the approved prototype, and these editorial instructions.

Do not simply accept AI-generated text without reviewing it.

You are responsible for verifying the information, evaluating the writing, and editing the final result.

AI should assist your work, not replace your judgment.

4. Follow the Approved PDP Structure

Use the Norix Prodigy Bed PDP as the primary example.

A. Product Title (H1)

Include the manufacturer, product series, descriptive product name, and model number where appropriate.

Example:

Norix Prodigy Anti-Ligature Bed – PRD100 / PRD100B

Avoid unnecessary punctuation, redundant terms, and overly long product titles.

B. Product Subtitle

Use a short subtitle describing the product's primary function, application, or distinguishing characteristics.

The subtitle should complement the H1 rather than repeat it.

C. Opening Product Description

Write one or two concise paragraphs explaining:

  • What the product is
  • Who it is intended for
  • Its most important benefits or distinguishing characteristics
  • Important configurations or options, if applicable

Use clear, professional language appropriate for institutional purchasing managers.

Avoid exaggerated marketing claims and unnecessary repetition.

D. Product Features and Specifications

Following the introduction, include a combined bullet list of relevant product-specific features and technical specifications.

Do not add a separate "Key Features" or "Specifications" heading unless the product requires one for clarity.

Format bullets with a bold label followed by a colon.

Example:

  • Construction: One-piece, reinforced polymer
  • Overall Dimensions: 38” W × 82” L × 18.5” H
  • Available Finishes: Harvest, Biscotti, Java

Include information relevant to the individual product. Do not add generic specifications simply to make the list longer.

Avoid duplicating features that are already explained adequately in the Prodigy Series section.

E. Available Models and Options

If a product has multiple model numbers, configurations, or important options, clearly identify them.

Use a short, bold subheading where appropriate.

For products with only one model and no meaningful options, this section may be omitted.

F. Applications (H2)

Describe the environments where the product is intended to be used.

Relevant markets may include:

  • Behavioral healthcare facilities
  • Psychiatric hospitals and inpatient psychiatric units
  • Residential treatment centers
  • Correctional and detention facilities
  • Other appropriate institutional environments

Do not automatically list every market for every product. Applications must be appropriate to the specific product and supported by the manufacturer information.

Include an appropriate internal link to the relevant Anchortex product collection.

G. About Norix Prodigy Bedroom Furniture Series (H2)

Use the approved Prodigy Series description to explain the characteristics shared by the product family.

This section should describe the series as a whole, including its design, construction, safety-related characteristics, TruGrain technology, and other common features.

The series description may be reused across the appropriate Prodigy PDPs.

Do not unnecessarily repeat the same series information in the product-specific description.

Do not add comparisons between individual products unless there is a meaningful difference that customers would not otherwise recognize.

H. About Norix Furniture (H2)

Use the approved, brief Norix manufacturer description.

Include a link to the Anchortex Norix collection:

https://www.anchortex.com/collections/norix-furniture

This section should remain consistent across Norix PDPs.

5. SEO and GEO Guidelines

Write primarily for customers who are researching and purchasing institutional furniture.

Use relevant search terms naturally. Depending on the product, these may include:

  • Behavioral health furniture
  • Behavioral healthcare furniture
  • Psychiatric hospital furniture
  • Ligature-resistant furniture
  • Anti-ligature furniture
  • Institutional bedroom furniture
  • Correctional and detention furniture

Do not add keywords that are not relevant to the product.

Additional guidelines:

  • Use descriptive, accurate product names and headings
  • Include manufacturer model numbers where relevant
  • Present specifications clearly
  • Explain important product differences and options
  • Avoid keyword stuffing
  • Avoid repetitive descriptions
  • Use factual, specific language instead of generic promotional statements

The goal is to make each PDP useful to both customers and search engines, including AI-driven product discovery.

6. Meta Titles and Descriptions

Prepare a meta title and meta description for each PDP.

Meta titles:

  • Include the manufacturer and important product keywords
  • Keep titles concise, generally aiming for approximately 50–65 characters when practical
  • Use "at Anchortex" at the end when space permits
  • Avoid unnecessary punctuation or keyword repetition

Meta descriptions:

  • Generally aim for approximately 140–160 characters
  • Identify the product and its most important benefits or applications
  • Write natural, useful descriptions that encourage relevant customers to visit the page
  • Avoid unsupported claims such as "best price" or "safest" unless substantiated

These lengths are guidelines, not absolute limits.

7. Shopify Formatting

Follow the formatting of the approved prototype.

  • Use the product title as H1
  • Use H2 headings for Applications, About Prodigy Series, and About Norix
  • Use bullet points for features and specifications
  • Bold specification labels followed by a colon
  • Do not use periods at the end of bullet points
  • Avoid unnecessary bold text in paragraphs
  • Remove unnecessary <span> tags
  • Do not place <p> tags inside bullet-point <li> elements
  • Avoid excessive paragraph spacing
  • Use internal links only where relevant
  • Keep the HTML clean and consistent

Review the rendered Shopify PDP, not just the HTML editor, to confirm that the formatting displays correctly.

8. Quality Control Before Completion

Before submitting each PDP, verify that:

  • Product information matches manufacturer documentation
  • Model numbers, specifications, dimensions, and available options are accurate
  • Safety and certification statements are supported
  • Descriptions are readable, concise, and free of unnecessary repetition
  • Product-specific information is distinguished from series-wide characteristics
  • H1, subtitle, and metadata are optimized
  • Headings, bullets, and HTML formatting are correct
  • Internal links are accurate and functional
  • The finished page follows the approved prototype

Use your judgment to improve the content when necessary.

If you encounter unusual product information, conflicting specifications, or a situation not covered by the prototype, flag it for review.

The Norix Evvolve Table pairs a ribbed, rotationally molded polyethylene base with a laminate or DuoForm™ top, for dayrooms, dining areas, activity rooms, and visitation areas in behavioral healthcare and correctional facilities. It comes in 36”, 42”, and 48” round and 36” and 42” square sizes, with a plain laminate top or a game top. The 42” square size is also available with a molded DuoForm™ top.

Norix designed the ribbed base around rotational symmetry, a pattern linked to a calm, comforting environment. The tapered shape adds stability when the base is ballasted and leaves room to clean around it. The base is made to be weighted on site with 200 lbs of dry sand or gravel, or Norix can ballast it at the factory. Ballast or a bolt-down kit is required to validate the warranty.

  • Top Attachment: Embedded T-nuts give the laminate top a steel-to-steel connection to the base
