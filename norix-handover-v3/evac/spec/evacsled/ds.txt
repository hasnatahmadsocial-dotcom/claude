                                                      THE RESQME ENABLES CARERS TO RAPIDLY
                                                      AND SAFELY EVACUATE PEOPLE WITH MOBILITY
                                                      PROBLEMS IN AN EMERGENCY.
                                                      The ResQme is an evacuation board designed to help
                                                      evacuate non-ambulant people or people who may not be
                                                      able to walk down stairs in an emergency. It can be used
                                                      for children to adults 6 foot 6 inches (198cm.) tall.
                                                      The ResQme can be used in office buildings, theatres,
                                                      libraries, hospitals, schools and many other facilities.
