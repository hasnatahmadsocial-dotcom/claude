DuraVision polycarbonate mirrors from Norix are designed specifically for the challenging conditions found in correctional and mental health facilities. The strength of polycarbonate combined with a solid, full foam backing and a unique steel frame design ensures these mirrors exceed the breakability and tamper resistance of any other product on the market.

Most convex mirrors you see in commercial establishments and at intersections are made from acrylic. When tested to withstand impact, they will be the first to break into knife-like shards. Duravision polycarbonate mirrors, with pure reflective coatings, are premium 3mm grade with a tensile strength of 9,400 psi. To further enhance the strength, the cavity behind the mirror is filled with high-density water blown urethane foam to limit the compression distance, thus eliminating the possibility of breakage from a severe blow. To make sure they stay in place, the DuraVision mirror perimeter is fully enclosed in a heavy duty powder coated steel frame that sits flush with the wall and ceiling and features countersunk screw holes to receive tamper-resistant fasteners.

  • Detention quality polycarbonate convex mirror with welded steel frame and rigid foam backing.
  • Engineered and proven suitable for continuous daily use in an institutional environment.
