The Norix Progress chair is a passive ergonomic stackable chair with a live hinge molded into the backrest. This commercial chair is designed to supply a high level of back comfort in both upright and relaxed sitting positions. With or without upholstery, the Progress chair looks good in any environment. With your choice of 4 colors plus lots of optional upholstery options, you have an attractive combination to accent a training room, a classroom, a lobby or an office.

  • Molded high impact grade co-polymer polypropylene seat and back with antistatic inhibitor added
  • Also available in upholstered version - contact us for more information
  • Ideal for staff use, classrooms and training rooms
  • Passes ANSI/BIFMA X5.1 2002 General Purpose Office Chairs. Not CALTB133 Compliant.
  • Black powder coated aluminum legs
  • Dimensions: 32"Hx23-1/4"Dx19-3/4"W, 10 lbs
  • Colors: Twilight, Cove, Sand, Dove
