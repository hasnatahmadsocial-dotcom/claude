Norix Boulder Series Beam Seats have long been in use in lobbies and waiting areas
throughout the world because they’re built to last, with powder coated tubular steel frames, welded steel plate seat supports, easy to clean molded polyethylene seats, heavy-duty glides and 9 tamper-resistant screws to hold each seat in place.

  • Norix Boulder Series Beam Seat bench with arms at ends (also available in models with out arms)
  • Fabrication & Material:
    • Base Frame: Tubular steel, bent and welded to provide arm or armless in-line seating. Standard configurations for 2 to 4 people. Flat steel plate welded to the frame at each seat location to provide strength, support, and a mounting platform on which the seat is attached. 2" diameter, 14 gauge steel with 11 gauge steel seat supports.
    • Seats: One-piece seat and back. Smooth maintenance free surface allows for easy cleaning. Molded-in threaded steel inserts provide for tamper resistant attachment to base frame. Rotationally molded, specially formulated, high-impact polyethylene with ultraviolet light stabilizers (to reduce fading). Chemically resistant to blood, vinegar, urine, feces, salt solution and chlorine solution. Premium TruColor™ polymer is fully compounded for superior color consistency and durability.
  • Dimensions: Seat Height 18", Seat Width 17", Seat Depth 16", Overall Height 31.4", Depth 25", Arm Height 24"
  • Models:
    • C213-2 - 2 seats, width 44", weight 87 lbs
    • C213-3 - 3 seats, width 65", weight 118 lbs
    • C213-4 - 4 seats, width 86", weight 153 lbs
  • Installation: use free-standing or bolt-down configuration (bolt-down hardware included for fastening to floor)
  • Frame color: Black
  • Seat colors: Twilight, Cove, Sage, Clay, Sand, Dove
  • Made in the U.S.A.
