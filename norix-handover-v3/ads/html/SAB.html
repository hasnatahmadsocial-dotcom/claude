Overcrowded facilities and emergency temporary bunking can be a serious problem if you are no prepared. Most courts have made it unlawful to sleep inmates on the floor. The Norix Stack-A-Bunk solves the problem with a washable, stackable sleep surface that doesn't have component parts which can be turned into a weapon. The Stack-A-Bunk is molded in one-piece from high-impact, polyethylene. It passes the California Technical Bulletin 133 Flammability Test, and you can stack 22 of them in a 6 ft. high space. The standard size fits under most existing steel beds and both sizes can be used with or without a mattress. The non-absorbent polyethylene is easily washed or steam cleaned to keep it free of germs or blood borne pathogens.
