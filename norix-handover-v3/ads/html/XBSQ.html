Norix heavy duty X base style tables are engineered and proven suitable for continuous daily use in a healthcare or correctional environment. Available in a variety of colors to suit your environment, with solid top or game top. X style base is meant to define each person's space.

  • Tabletop sizes 36" or 42" square
  • Fabrication and Material:
    • Base: Two interlocking base panels. 1 5/8" thick 45 lb density particle board, sandwiched between 1/8" thick hardboard and faced both sides with .050" thick, high-pressure laminate and banded on three exposed edges with heavy duty T-molding that is glued and steel pinned every 12". An intermediate mounting plate joins the base to the top.
    • Top: Premium M3 Grade, 45 lb. density particle board table top core is sandwiched between 1/8" thick hardboard and faced with special 0.05” thick high pressure laminate for superior quality and long term durability. Molded edge is radiused for safety and comfort, pressure fused to the top, rendering it firmly attached and highly sanitary. Embedded T-nuts secure top to base with tight steel to steel connection for maximum long term reliability. Robust 1.5" thick edge profile.
  • Top and edge color choices:
    • Black edge + Titanium Evolve top
    • Wild Cherry edge + Wild Cherry top
    • Kensington Maple edge + Kensington Maple top
    • Asian Sun edge + Asian Sun top
    • Montana Walnut edge + Montana Walnut top
    • Graham Cracker edge + Palisades Oak top
    • HM Innertone Light edge + Calcutta Marble top
    • Black edge + Black Alicante top
    • Black edge + Titanium Evolve Game Top
  • Base type and color: Molded, Black
  • Models:
    • XB3636 - 36"L x 36"W x 29.3"H, 139 lbs
    • XB4242 - 42"L x 42"W x 29.3"H, 162 lbs
  • Installation: none required
  • Heavy weight requires no ballast


About Norix Furniture:

Norix humanizes behavioral and correctional facilities, while building extreme durability and security into each piece of institutional furniture so it stands up to correctional grade use. Norix furniture is as tough as your prison or detention grade environment, yet safe for trustees and staff. Anchortex supplies the complete line of Norix furniture for patient rooms, classrooms, inmate cells, day rooms, lobbies, intake areas, chapels, dining halls, outdoor/recreation, contact visitation and video-visitation areas, as well as public safety furniture for police and fire stations that looks good and stands up to hard use.
