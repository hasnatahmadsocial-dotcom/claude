About Norix Attenda Bedroom Series:
Behavioral healthcare professionals have long recognized that traditional furniture is completely unsuitable for their critical environments. Norix Attenda furniture is designed to provide the strength, safety and cleanability required for the daily rigors of institutional living — in a sleek, contemporary look.

Safe, Secure...And Built to Last -- The key to Norix Attenda’s strength and durability is its advanced construction. Molded in specially formulated, high-impact polyethylene, each Norix Attenda product is structurally enhanced for increased strength and durability. Solid as a rock, yet featuring seamless construction with rounded corners for added safety and security. Each piece is designed to be attached flush to the wall or floor. Attenda’s rugged, exterior skin is seamless and impervious to liquids, including bodily fluids and industrial cleaners. The non-absorbent, polymer construction simply leaves no place for fluids to go. Chronic maintenance problems are washed away.
