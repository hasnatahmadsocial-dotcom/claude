Product Description

The Comfort Shield® Foam Mattress is an institutional-grade dorm mattress with antimicrobial nylon cover and anti-crevice inverted seam. These dorm mattresses is available in two grade levels: economy Foam Core construction, and a Dual Comfort Foam Encased Innerspring construction. Comfort Shield® Sewn Sealed Mattresses carry a 3 year limited warranty, and comply with current flammability test standards.

Anti-crevice inverted seam construction uses fire retardant Kevlar® thread for strength and durability. Anti-microbial 210 Denier Oxford Nylon Cover, with polyurethane coating and laminated flame retardant barrier is moisture resistant, anti-bacterial, crack resistant and stain resistant. Available in two grade levels: Foam Core Construction and Dual Comfort foam Encased Innerspring.
