Product Description

Built for connection and comfort, the Double Pedestal Table brings people together while respecting individual space. Its generous proportions support up to eight users with plenty of room to dine, work, or gather comfortably. The clean pedestal design keeps layouts simple and reduces visual clutter, while ADA-compliant accessibility ensures easy access for wheelchairs and other mobility aides – creating an inclusive table designed for everyone.

Toggle Marker
Neuro-Aesthetic Design: The simple dimensional pattern and rotational symmetry of the base promote positive emotional and psychological experiences among residents.
